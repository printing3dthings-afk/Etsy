# Media library

Every photo and video the shop owns, by build. Generated by `tools/media_library.py`; `library.json` has one record per file.

**The tag is what a post must say** (the shop's first rule): REAL is Scott's own photo or footage; EDITED is his photo with an AI-made background; RENDER is a design render, never a photo; SIMULATION is drawn from the sliced toolpath; VIDEO is a finished video of ours, with its own SCRIPT.md; LISTING is a digital product's listing image. Other sellers' images (`data/design_references`) are left out: research only, never ours to post.

- **Total:** 1901 files: 69 REAL, 4 EDITED, 866 RENDER, 557 SIMULATION, 85 VIDEO, 320 LISTING.
- **Builds:** 140.

## Adding to it

- **Scott's photos and clips** go in the build's own `real/` folder (`real/photos/` for stills), with a line in its `real/README.md`: what it shows and when.
- **Design images** go in the build's `images/`, named for what they show (`_section`, `_exploded`, `_as_printed`, `_v2`), so they're tagged right here.
- **Design-process videos** keep a still from each step in `stills/` beside the video (`storyboard.save_stills`); add the video to `DESIGN_VIDEOS` in the tool so it files under its build.
- Then re-run `python3 tools/media_library.py`.
