# TikTok: the OBC desk sign

Started by Claude, 2026-11-11, after Scott said yes to a TikTok of his logo sign.

- **Where:** TikTok (@onbrandcraftz). It can be reused as an Instagram Reel or
  YouTube Short.
- **Size:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, MP4 (H.264). Keep text out of the top 220 px and
  the bottom 25%, where the app's buttons and caption sit.
- **Length:** 15 to 20 seconds.
- **What it must show:** the OBC desk sign. It's Scott's gold OBC logo, traced
  from the logo itself, raised on a black plaque that slides into a black base.
  Use only these real sources, all on the branch `claude/new-session-o245bl`
  in `openscad_models/brand/obc_desk_sign/`:
  - `images/obc_desk_sign_front.png`, `_three_quarter.png`, `_top.png`: 3D
    renders of the exported parts.
  - `images/obc_desk_sign_as_printed.png`: rendered from the sliced toolpath.
  - `images/guide_parts.png`, `guide_insert.png`: the two prints, and the plaque
    going into the slot.
  - The images are in Git LFS: `git lfs pull -I "<path>"` after fetching the branch.
- **Facts you may use** (source: `OBC_DESK_SIGN_PRINTING.md` and
  `checks/slices.txt` in that folder):
  - Plaque 182 × 88 mm, letters 150 mm wide, standing 2.4 mm proud.
  - Base 196 × 46 × 14 mm. The plaque leans back about 10°.
  - Two prints: the plaque (black, then gold, one colour change) about 2 h 23 m;
    the base about 1 h 36 m.
- **Must not say:**
  - **That it has been printed.** It hasn't yet. Every image is a render or a
    toolpath simulation, and must be labelled as one on screen.
  - **That it's for sale or free.** It's Scott's own sign, not a listing.
  - **Anything about the logo beyond the logo itself.** The logo isn't redrawn.
- **Text:** your call. Claude's entry uses "Turning my logo into a 3D print".
- **Needed:** whenever both entries are in; Scott picks.

When Scott has printed it, his real footage beats any of this. That would be part two.
