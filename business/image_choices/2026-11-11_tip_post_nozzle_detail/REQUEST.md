# Tip post: what a 0.4 mm nozzle can really print

Started by Claude, 2026-11-11. Scott sent examples of tip carousels and reels he
wants us to match ("We need to get on the level of other people's post"); see
the animation thread for what they have in common.

- **Where:** Instagram (post or carousel, plus the reel), Facebook, Pinterest. The
  reel also works on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- **Sizes:** post 1080 × 1350 (4:5); reel 1080 × 1920 (9:16), MP4 H.264. In the
  reel, keep text between y 225 and y 1440: the apps' top bar sits above that, and
  the caption and buttons sit in the bottom quarter.
- **The idea:** one question, six numbered answers, each with a small picture. Ends
  with "save this" and a source line. Black and gold, the shop's own colours.
- **Facts you may use:** only these six, all measured on our own detail test,
  from `openscad_models/detail_calibration/DETAIL_STANDARD.md` on the branch
  `claude/new-session-o245bl`:
  1. A 0.4 mm groove running up a wall kept its depth at every layer height tried.
  2. At 0.2 mm layers, a 0.3 mm groove printed 0.4. Heights snap to whole layers.
  3. A 0.1 mm groove printed as a whole 0.2 mm layer: twice the size drawn.
  4. A 1 mm dome is just 5 layers at 0.2 mm: it prints as stairs.
  5. Letters need to be about 3 mm tall on a 0.4 nozzle; smaller ones blob or lose strokes.
  6. Dots on a top face need to be about 0.8 mm across; smaller ones are dropped, not shrunk.
- **Must not say:**
  - That these hold for every printer or filament. They're our P1S, our tests.
  - Anything that sells. It's a free tip post; a link to the shop in the caption is fine.
- **The OBC logo:** use it as it is, never redrawn.
