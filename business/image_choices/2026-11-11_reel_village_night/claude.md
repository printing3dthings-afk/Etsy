# Claude's reel

`claude.mp4`: 1080 × 1920, 10 s, 30 fps, no audio (add music on your side).

**What happens:** snow falls on three real Gingerbread village buildings. Their
windows light up one at a time: cottage, then chapel, then cocoa café. The camera
eases in.

**Made from:** the STL files inside the Gingerbread sale ZIP, in the README
colours, rendered in Blender (EEVEE). The glow comes through the plastic: thin
white window panes glow brightest, as the closed panes of the real print would.
Every frame says "3D animation of the design files".

**Known flaw for v2:** a hard horizon line where the snow ground ends.

**Source:** `business/social/animations/village_night/` on the branch
`claude/new-session-o245bl`.
