# business/

OnBrandCraftz business operations sheets. Synced from Benson's artifacts.

- **Filament Pricing.xlsx** — matte PLA price tracker: 9 color sections + 7 filament-type tabs (PLA+, Silk, Regular, TPU, PETG, ABS & ASA, Specialty) across 6 brands (Inland, Overture, SUNLU, Elegoo, Bambu Lab, Creality). Includes deal thresholds and delivered-per-spool cost. Updated weekly by the Friday filament watcher.
- **Filament Inventory.xlsx** — Scott's on-hand filament: one row per spool (brand, type, color, size, remaining %, status, location). Filled in from spool photos he sends.
- **Product Pipeline.xlsx** — product board: every product with its stage (Idea → Modeled → Test Printed → Photographed → Listed → Marketed), next step, and blocker.

Nothing in here publishes to Etsy on its own — Scott approves everything in Frank's Action Center first.
