# Daily meetings: Muse and Claude

**Scott, 2026-11-11:** "I want you two to have a daily meeting to help each other
grow the business. Also a second meeting daily to figure out my finances."

They're written meetings: one file per meeting, each of us writes our part, and
the file closes with decisions and actions. Times are Indiana time (Eastern).

## 1. Growth meeting, every day

File: `growth/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`. Whoever arrives first copies `TEMPLATE_growth.md`.

- **Muse's part, by 12:00:**
  - what went out since the last meeting, with real numbers from the dashboards;
  - what's working and what isn't;
  - what's planned today;
  - what Muse needs from Claude.
- **Claude's part, at 1:17 pm** (a scheduled session):
  - the design pipeline: what's READY, what's in progress, what's coming;
  - fact sheets and media kits owed, and reviews owed;
  - answers to what Muse needs;
  - one or two proposals to grow sales or reach.
- **Close, by Claude in the same session:**
  - **Decisions:** only within rules Scott has already set.
  - **Actions:** owner, what, by when.
  - **For Scott:** anything that needs his call, in plain words, each with a
    recommendation.

  Muse picks up its actions on its next run.

**The rules don't change in a meeting:**
- Nothing is posted, published, priced, bought or sent without Scott, as now.
- A meeting can plan work and ask Scott. It can't approve on his behalf.

**If either side misses a day,** the other still writes its part and the actions,
and says so at the top.

## 2. Finance meeting, every day: in a private repository

**This repository is public**, so the finance meeting doesn't live here. Scott chose
his private repository `printing3dthings-afk/etsy-store`, folder `finance/`, for
business and personal finances (2026-11-11). Its README has the times and rules.
In short:
- Muse's part by 6:00 pm, Claude's part and the close at 8:43 pm.
- Figures only from Scott or real statements, never guessed.
- No account numbers.
- Nothing moved, paid or signed up for.

**Never copy anything from that folder into this repository.**

Muse: if you can't reach that repository, say so in a thread here, and Scott can
add access. Claude holds the meeting alone until then.
