# Standards we both follow

Status: **agreed (Scott)** is Scott's own rule. **Agreed** means both of us
accepted it in a thread. **Proposed** waits for the other's answer.

## Agreed (Scott)

1. **Never lie to the customer.** Every photo, number and claim is true and
   checked (CLAUDE.md, top rule).
2. **Show only the real product.** Renders or photos of our own files, labelled as
   renders wherever someone could take them for a photo. No AI stand-ins.
3. **Say "free" only when that file really is free on that platform.**
4. **The logo is never changed or redrawn** (Scott, 2026-11-11).
5. **Post and MakerWorld images:** each of us makes one and Scott picks
   (`business/image_choices/`, Scott 2026-11-11).
6. **Muse logs every post** in `data/social/muse_log.md`, append-only.
7. **No other brands' logos or characters,** and no copies of other makers' designs.
8. **Nothing is posted, published or sent to a customer without Scott's
   approval,** except what he has already set up to run.

## Agreed (Muse and Claude, 2026-11-11, `threads/2026-11-11_start.md`)

9. **Every number names its source file,** in the log's "Claims made" line: the
   README, printing notes, slice or FACTS.md it came from. *Why:* the village
   READMEs give one-colour times about a third of the older listing drafts.
10. **Show the newest version.** Check the design's folder for a v2 or v3 first.
    *Why:* Santa's Workshop v1 renders were still going round while v2 was the sale file.
11. **No "buy on Etsy" while the shop is down,** words burned into videos and
    images included. The exception is Scott's own: YouTube A/B Tests 7 and 8 keep
    their Etsy endings (approved 2026-11-09).
12. **A READY line when a design is finished.** Claude posts "READY: <design>
    v<n> — fact sheet: <path>, media kit: <path>" in a thread. Until then a folder
    is work in progress, and Muse doesn't upload or post it.
13. **A version-in-progress flag.** While Claude works on a v<n+1> of something
    live or queued, the design folder carries a line saying so, and Muse holds the
    old version until Scott picks.
14. **Checked before Scott sees it.** Profiles use checked slices, posts pass a
    claim check against the design's FACTS.md, and must-fixes are settled here
    before anything reaches Scott.
15. **Every design gets a FACTS.md** in its folder: version and what changed,
    print time and grams per profile from the slice, outside size, free or paid
    per platform, a "safe to say" list, which render shows which version, and
    light fit where it applies.

## Agreed (Scott, 2026-11-14)

16. **Everything either of us does moves the business forward.** Scott: "Make sure
    everything you and Benson do is always in the greater good of moving the
    business forward." The test, before starting any piece of work: *which of these
    does it move, and by when: a sale, reach that leads to a sale, or a product
    that can be sold?* If the answer is none, don't do it, or say so and ask Scott.
    - **Deadlines first.** Work with a real clock (Tin Clicker push by Nov 28, the
      physical-order cutoff Dec 12, Valentine listings Jan 3) goes ahead of work
      without one.
    - **Finish before starting.** A test print Scott can run, a fix to something
      already in the shop, or a delivered plan beats a new idea, new research or a
      new design.
    - **Say when something isn't worth doing,** including Scott's own asks and our
      own routines. Agreeing to be agreeable doesn't move anything.
    - **Not at any cost:** standards 1–15 still hold. A sale built on a false claim
      doesn't count as moving forward.

17. **Private details go in the private folder, never here.** This repo is public.
    Billing or payment problems, anything personal about Scott, unannounced prices and
    any customer, supplier or creditor details go in `private/NOTES.md` in the private
    repo `printing3dthings-afk/etsy-store` (rules in `private/README.md` there). This repo
    gets one neutral line at most, e.g. "Etsy's return depends on Scott." If something
    private lands here, remove it in the next commit and move it there. History is not
    rewritten (Scott, 2026-11-14).
