MakerWorld" only if that video's building is actually public on MakerWorld @P3DT. For

## Method / evidence

- Subjects and old IDs: MANIFEST.md Table A; new IDs: upload-results.md.
- @P3DT inventory: the 19-model authoritative list (Nov 13–14). Cross-checked against the
  Nov 13 listing verification already appended to MANIFEST.md — this audit agrees with it on
  every single-building row, and adds the building-by-building checks it did not do.
- #27's eight subjects read from the re-exported file's own on-screen segment labels
  ("1 OF 8"–"8 OF 8" + end card "8 PRINT FILES ON MAKERWORLD", frames extracted and viewed).
- #28's fifteen subjects from its build spec (2026-10-24), which lists all 15 registry singles
  by name and source file.
- #29's three subjects from its build caption record (Drum, Gift Box, Hex).
- #26: frames extracted and viewed — the tour carries the persistent footer throughout and
  includes a building with on-model "INN" signage (the Coaching Inn, not on @P3DT). A complete
  VO-by-VO building list for this video does not exist in local records; one confirmed
  non-@P3DT building is sufficient to fail the blanket claim.
- #8/#18 variant call rests on the standing record that @P3DT 3518521 is the Victorian
  Santa's Workshop and the gingerbread variant is a separate paid design.
- Unidentified rows: none — every row's subject is named in the manifest or its build records.
