## TikTok, Instagram reel, Facebook reel, YouTube Short
Four tops, one print, four ironing settings. Which is smoothest?

A: no ironing
B: 0.10 mm spacing, 10% flow, 15 mm/s
C: 0.15 mm spacing, 10% flow, 30 mm/s
D: 0.20 mm spacing, 15% flow, 20 mm/s

C won, and C is Bambu Studio's stock ironing. So our rule: every flat face a customer sees gets
ironed.
