# Video queue

Claude makes every social video (STANDARDS 21, Scott 2026-11-20). Each finished video gets a
folder here, `<post date>_<series>_<slug>/`, holding:
- `final.mp4`, or one file per platform cut (`ig.mp4`, `tiktok.mp4`, `yt.mp4` and so on);
- `CAPTION.md`, the caption draft per platform;
- `FACTS.md`, every claim on screen or in the caption, with its source.

Scott approves the video. Benson runs the claim check, posts it and logs it in `muse_log.md`.
`_inbox/` is for footage and sounds Benson or Scott hand to Claude.
