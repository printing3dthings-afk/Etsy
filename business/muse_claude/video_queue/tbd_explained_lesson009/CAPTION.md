## TikTok, Instagram reel, Facebook reel, YouTube Short
Why does an ocarina have different-sized holes? We printed a test whistle to find out.

All holes closed: 1478 Hz.
5 mm hole open: 1822 Hz.
8 mm hole open: 2125 Hz.

The bigger the open hole, the higher the note. That's why every hole on an ocarina is its own size.
