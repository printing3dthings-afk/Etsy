## Instagram reel + carousel, Facebook reel + carousel
🎓 Lesson 007: draw a 0.3 mm groove, get 0.4.

Your printer builds in 0.2 mm layers, so anything that runs level snaps to a whole layer. We
sliced our own detail test coupon and measured every feature. A 0.3 mm groove came out 0.4. A
0.1 mm groove took a full layer: twice the size we drew. And a 1 mm dome? Five stacked layers.

Lines that run UP the face are different. The nozzle places those sideways, not in steps. Every
upright groove we tested, 0.1 to 1.0 mm deep, came out within 0.01 mm.

The 0.2 mm rule: run fine detail up the face, and put heights on the 0.2 mm grid.

(Measured on the sliced toolpath of our test coupon at 0.20 mm layers.)

Save this for your next detail print. What's the smallest detail you've got to print clean?
