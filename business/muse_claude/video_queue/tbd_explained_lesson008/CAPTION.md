## Instagram reel + carousel, Facebook reel + carousel
🎓 Lesson 008: what 100% infill really costs.

Lesson 006 said skip 100% infill. So we sliced our own Gingerbread Cottage three ways in Bambu
Studio. Same house, same printer; only the walls and infill change:
• 2 walls, 100% infill: 113 g, 3 h 04 m
• 5 walls, 20% infill: 64 g, 2 h 24 m
• Ours, 3 walls, 15% infill: 51 g, 2 h 15 m

That's less than half the plastic of the solid one, and about 50 minutes quicker.

Where's the strength? In the walls. That was Lesson 006 (CNC Kitchen's pull tests, via
@3dmartos).

Before you print solid, slice it both ways.

Send this to the maker who prints everything at 100%.
