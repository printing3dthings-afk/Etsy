## Instagram post + reel, Facebook, Pinterest (the post), TikTok, YouTube Short
What can a 0.4 mm nozzle really print? We measured it on our own detail test:

1. Lines that run up a wall hold. A 0.4 mm groove kept its depth at every layer height we tried.
2. Heights snap to layers. At 0.2 mm layers, a 0.3 mm groove printed 0.4.
3. Too small gets bigger. A 0.1 mm groove printed as a whole 0.2 mm layer.
4. Small domes are stairs. A 1 mm dome is just 5 layers at 0.2 mm.
5. Letters 3 mm tall. Smaller ones blob or lose strokes.
6. Dots 0.8 mm across. Smaller dots aren't shrunk, they're dropped.

Measured from the sliced toolpath, on a 0.4 mm nozzle. Save this for your next detail print.
