## Findings (with sources — full reads)

### 1. YOUTUBE IMAGE POSTS NOW LIVE IN THE SHORTS FEED — OUR CAROUSELS HAVE A YOUTUBE SURFACE WE'VE NEVER USED [NEW for us — announced Sep 23 / expanded Jun 30, 2026; not covered in any prior Thursday note]
- YouTube's own announcement (TeamYouTube, Jun 30, 2026, via the Made on YouTube Sep 23 deep-dive): image posts and carousels now appear **in the Shorts feed** on iPhone and Android, and on channel pages on TV (rolling out). Specs: **up to 10 images** per carousel, text overlays on images, and **up to 15 seconds of music** (licensed/popular library, YouTube Audio Library royalty-free tracks, or Dream Track where eligible). Measurement rule stated by YouTube: **only image posts shown in the Shorts feed count as views**, reported under the Posts content type in Studio (posts live under the Posts tab, not Videos/Shorts).
  Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/464270211/new-features-for-the-youtube-era-made-on-youtube-2026?hl=th-TH · https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/446107479/new-ways-to-connect-with-your-audience-using-image-posts (search-read; the thread page itself renders empty to text fetch — content taken from YouTube's indexed announcement text, flagged)
- The access constraint (Profaily's Nov 24, 2026 check, read in full): image posts are created **in the YouTube mobile app only**; schedulers (Buffer/Metricool/Publer) cannot create them, and the new Data API `posts` resource (Nov 16, 2026) is **pilot-only for select launch partners**, general availability planned 2027. So this leg is manual for now — same class as Instagram carousel music (app-only, Nov 13 standard).
  Source: https://useprofaily.com/blog/how-to-post-images-to-youtube
- **Gap for us:** Our format ratio rule (5 video : 1 carousel) governs *production*; it says nothing about *syndication*. The Versus Tue/Fri swipe carousel and Explained carousels are already built, already carry licensed-music plans on IG, and already have a YouTube audience that is our strongest platform — yet YouTube only ever receives the video version. A ≤10-slide deck fits the YouTube cap exactly (longer decks would need a cut, which is a real constraint: our 11-slide decks don't fit unedited). This is reuse of a finished asset on a new feed surface, not new carousel production — the ratio is untouched.

### 2. INSTAGRAM NOW TAGS AMAZON AFFILIATE LINKS NATIVELY ON REELS AND FEED POSTS (Nov 16) — BUT IT RUNS ON AN *INFLUENCER* ACCOUNT, NOT OUR ASSOCIATES TAG [NEW, Nov 2026]
- EmbedSocial's November log (read in full tonight): US creators can add Amazon affiliate links to Reels and feed posts/carousels; after connecting an **Amazon Influencer account**, Instagram auto-adds the links when Amazon products are tagged; Mosseri states Instagram takes **no cut**. Limits: **up to 30 links on a Reel, 20 on a feed post**; products can be tagged directly on items in photos. Instagram told TechCrunch product links on Reels/posts get **about twice as many clicks as a bio link**.
  Source: https://embedsocial.com/blog/new-instagram-features-2026/
- Corroboration with the eligibility detail (ProSMM, published Nov 17, 2026, read in full): "Since November 16, eligible creators with an Amazon Influencer account can add Amazon affiliate links to Reels and feed posts, up to 30 per Reel, and Instagram takes no share of the commission."
  Source: https://prosmm.io/blog/your-algorithm-instagram-facebook-2026
- **Gap for us — with the caveat stated plainly:** Our Amazon Associates is LIVE (tag onbrandcraftz-20, since Oct 31), but Associates ≠ Influencer: the Influencer Program is a separate qualification (social following review) that we have **not** verified we hold. So tonight's finding is an *eligibility check first*, not a wiring job. If eligible, this changes the geometry of our affiliate rule: links currently live links-page-first because Instagram had no native product tag for Amazon; a tagged product on the exact Reel that shows the filament/plate in use is the highest-intent placement we have. Two standing rules still bind any pilot: the verbatim affiliate disclosure in the caption, and Amazon's rule that we never hard-code Amazon prices into posts (Versus prices stay brand-store sourced).

### 3. STORIES CAN NOW BE EDITED FOR 15 MINUTES AFTER POSTING (Mosseri, Nov 18) — A REPAIR WINDOW FOR PROMO STORIES [NEW, small, operational]
- EmbedSocial (full read, Finding 2 source) and NapoleonCat's November log (search-read): Instagram refreshed Stories (new stickers/fonts, easier sticker sheet) and now lets users **edit a Story for 15 minutes after sharing it, without losing views, likes or replies**; announced by Mosseri Nov 18, 2026.
  Sources: https://embedsocial.com/blog/new-instagram-features-2026/ · https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-new-features-and-updates/?ref=halokatalks.com
- **Gap for us:** Our promo Stories carry a native link sticker to the exact video/product (Nov 13 standard) — the single most error-prone element we publish (wrong link = a dead promo). Until now a mistake meant delete-and-repost, losing accumulated views/replies. The 15-minute window converts that into a fix-in-place — *if* someone looks within 15 minutes. Our pipeline posts and verifies, but no step re-checks the Story inside that window specifically.

### Proposal 2 — Verify Amazon Influencer eligibility, then pilot ONE native product tag on a partner/product Reel
- **Specific change:** Step 1 (check only): confirm whether the OnBrandCraftz Amazon account qualifies for / already holds an Influencer account distinct from the live Associates tag onbrandcraftz-20 — report the answer before anything is wired. Step 2 (only if eligible): tag the actual Amazon-listed product shown in ONE already-planned Reel (e.g. a Panda on the Bench episode's plate, if Amazon-listed, or a SUNLU/Overture filament actually in frame), with the standing verbatim affiliate disclosure in the caption and no Amazon price stated anywhere in the post. - **Effort:** Low for the check; Low for the pilot tag. No change if eligibility fails — the links page remains the Amazon route and that result is reported as-is.

### Proposal 3 — Add a 15-minute Story check to the promo-Story step

After the window, the old rule stands unchanged.
- **Which series it lifts:** Every promo Story (MakerWorld Promo, product spotlights, partner episodes) — protects the Nov 13 link-sticker standard's most failure-prone step.
- **Effort:** Minimal — one timed re-check in an existing step; no new tools.
