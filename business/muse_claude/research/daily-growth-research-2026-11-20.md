## Verified findings (recent sources, read Nov 20)

1. **"They wanted $X — I printed it for $Y" is the niche's biggest video this week.** TrendReport's YouTube 3D-printing tracker (week of 2026-11-18): the #1 video is "Porsche wanted $2,000... So I 3D printed it for $20 instead" (Hayden Schreier Builds) at 17,018,684 views. On-topic demand is up 47% vs 27 days earlier (21.97M views/day); 86% of the 300 ranking videos are Shorts, 57% come from channels under 100K subs, 71% were uploaded in the last 30 days — small, new channels rank. Top titles share the word "prints" (8 of top 30). OBC adaptation: a lit village house's retail décor price vs OBC's own measured filament cost — honest numbers only, and it fits the existing Versus/product lane.
   Source: trendreport.com/topic/3d-printing/

2. **Hands-only clicker builds are the breakout maker format — and comments write the sequel.** 3DPrint Land's bitten-ice-cream clicker (keyboard switch inside a printed shell) passed 3.6M views in its first week (story dated Nov 16, 2026): hands only, no spoken explanation — print, assemble, repeated clicking is the whole video, and the click/glue sounds are what comments discuss. Commenters request the next clicker ("Can you make PS4 clicker?", "iPhone duo clicker") — a comment-driven sequel loop. OBC owns this exact product class: the Gingerbread Tin Clicker, contest entry, promo already approved, deadline Nov 28. A hands-only click reel (click = the audio, per the original-audio rule) ending on "which clicker next?" feeds both the contest push and the sequel loop.
   Source: trari.com/en/story/3dpriintland-38785/

3. **Format class beats topic: challenges/comedy/reactions out-pull tutorials ~3×; faceless/POV out-pulls on-camera.** Revid's analysis of 176,367 TikToks with 1,000+ likes (Oct 2025–Oct 2026): median views — challenges 182K, comedy 155K, reactions 105K, tutorials 62K; faceless 114K vs 67K one-person-on-camera; POV 107K vs selfie 48K. Caveat: vendor study of already-successful videos — a pattern, not proof of cause. OBC is already faceless/POV (good), but Explained/Blueprint sit in the weakest class (tutorial); the misconception-first opens already adopted Nov 17 move them toward reaction/myth-bust framing — keep that direction, don't add talking-head.
   Source: revid.ai/blog/short-form-video-trends

4. **YouTube now packages Shorts as series (seasons/episodes, rolled out Sept 2026).** OBC's numbered series (Print Cam Ep 00x, Versus Day N, Hot Plate Ep 00x) are already built for this — using the series packaging + a recognisable opener makes back-catalogue episodes surface together instead of as one-offs. Same source also notes 30%+ of recommended Reels in Q1 2026 were same-day posts (Meta) — freshness matters; OBC's daily cadence already covers this.
   Source: revid.ai/blog/short-form-video-trends

5. **Two business-account formats trending in Nov 2026 roundups map straight onto OBC stock.** "Process versus the Product" (SocialBee, via eksis.io Nov 2026 roundup): process footage → finished reveal — that is Print Cam's exact shape, validation not change. "Moodboard Stack" (TIME Marketing): 6–8 best pieces in one Reel, hook line only, NO sales copy in the video — fits a Cyber Week (Nov 23–Dec 1) village roundup Reel where the selling happens in caption/link, keeping the video itself sendable. Business accounts also can't use most trending commercial audio — OBC's original-audio rule already sidesteps this.
   Source: eksis.io/blog/november-2026-social-media-trends-for-brands
