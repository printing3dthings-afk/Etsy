### A. Contest strategy

- **Separate, winnable Spotlight Awards exist and @P3DT has not (visibly) claimed them:** a MakerWorld Community Award (200 points, 1 winner) and an Instagram Spotlight Award (200 points, 1 winner) for sharing the *creative journey* — inspiration, sketches, modelling process, challenges/solutions. MakerWorld route: use **#clickerfactory and #Contests**; Instagram route: use **#clickerfactory and #madeinmakerworld**; both must include a direct link to the published model. The hosts state selected posts/models "may also receive extra visibility through official MakerWorld promotion."

#### A2. The Tin Clicker listing itself is already contest-ready on copy — its gap is traffic, not the page
- The live listing (model 3560658) states the dual function plainly (storage tin + MX-switch clicker lid), lists **three profiles — Standard, Optimized, and Multicolor**, no supports, Bambu Handy path, full Bambu range compatibility, and "Entered in the Click Into Celebration contest on MakerWorld." ### C. Release cadence from the Sjack7 bench

#### C1. MakerWorld gives new models a launch-window exposure bump — cadence should be built around it
- Forum analysis by creators using Creator Center data: "The first few days always have more views since it seems MW gives new models more exposures (impressions) during that launch period," with three diagnostic rates — View Rate (views/impressions, tests the cover), Download Rate (downloads/views), Print Rate (prints/views) — and a recommendation to judge a cover over at least a full week. The same thread: "The algorithm seems to reward those posting models more frequently too." Source: https://forum.bambulab.com/t/how-can-i-increase-my-model-popularity/143523?page=4
- One high-volume creator reports a preferred launch window of "4pm to 6pm … GMT & UTC" and that missing it cost momentum; another replies the platform is 24-hour and advises A/B-testing your own times. This is anecdote, not a MakerWorld rule — treat time-of-day as something to test, not obey. Source: https://forum.bambulab.com/t/broken-yet-again-makerworld-uploads-for-models-profiles-are-slooow/130464
- ⚠️ Daily-publishing limit: Scott's own standing record (AGENTS.md, learned 2026-10-25) is that MakerWorld has a daily model-publishing limit per account — bulk drops in one day will hit it. (Internal record, not an external claim.)

#### C2. What cadence that implies for @P3DT (my inference from C1, labelled as such)
- With 19 public models and a 100+ deep private bench, @P3DT is not catalogue-starved; it is *launch-window-starved*. One model per launch window, spaced so each gets its own first-days bump + its own social post, beats dumping five the same day (they share one bump, one news cycle, and the daily limit).
- Practical shape: **2 public releases/week through the Christmas window** (e.g. Tue + Fri, matching the existing social rhythm), each a *free* village piece or a free teaser for a paid piece, each launched with: contest tag if eligible, collection add, cross-links to siblings, one MakerWorld Community post, and one keyword-comment social post. Stop the Christmas cadence by ~Dec 12 (Scott's Christmas order cutoff in standing memory) and pivot the bench to Valentine/lantern pieces in January rather than launching more Christmas after the buying window closes.
- Seasonal-timing support (weak source, directional only): a holiday-timing guide argues launching festive models *before* peak demand, and counter-positioning if competitors cluster in early November. Source: https://takecontrol.troybilt.com/how-soon-to-release-holiday-themed-models-on-makerworls/

#### C3. The 100-print threshold is the funnel's real milestone — and Exclusive conflicts with the free-sync strategy
- Exclusive Model Program eligibility requires **at least 100 total accumulated prints** across a creator's models; Exclusive models earn **+25%** points/boost value, Exclusive Points cash out at **$0.066/point from a $100 balance**, but a model in the main program must be **MakerWorld-only for at least 90 days** and removed from other platforms. The 14-Day Exclusive Launch variant (+10%) requires a model **never released anywhere before** and locks it for 14 days. Sources: https://makerstats.io/makerworld-exclusive-models/ and https://makerworld.com/en/exclusive-model-policy
- ⚠️ Strategy conflict Scott must decide (see CHOICE below): @P3DT's standing free-sync rule puts every free MakerWorld model also on Patreon (free) and Printables. That disqualifies those models from Exclusive. Exclusive is therefore only compatible with *brand-new, MakerWorld-first* designs (14-Day Launch), not with the existing free village set.

### D. Profile / gallery tactics top village & tea-light designers use

#### D1. Real-print photos are now a hard visibility rule, not a style preference
- MakerWorld's official Model Image Rule Update (effective **Feb 5, 2026**): "Model images must include at least one real printed image that matches the uploaded model file and model name… both model images and profile images must include real printed images." New models without one "may be restricted from visibility"; covers showing a full set when the files contain only part of it are a named violation ("Mismatch Between Images and Files"); render covers are tolerated only if shape/structure/proportions closely match the real print. Source (opened in full): https://makerworld.com/en/community/post/1447793
- A forum report from Oct 2026 describes the current upload flow as requiring "**Real life photos (required) — at least three views of the printed model**" as a separate category from optional model pictures. (Forum report, not the official post — the official post says "at least one.") Source: https://forum.bambulab.com/t/new-picture-requirements-awkward-for-existing-listings/260641
- This aligns with, and hardens, Scott's existing professional-cover standard: staged real-print cover, thumbnail-legible, real photo stays in gallery. Any Sjack7 bench model whose only imagery is renders is **not launchable** until it has been printed and photographed — cadence in C2 must be gated on photos, not files.

#### D2. The anatomy of a peer tea-light listing that converts (Santa's Workshop example)
- A directly comparable peer listing ("Santa's Workshop Light-Up House") uses: a benefit-led opening ("lights up from the inside… cozy window-glow"), a **Boost Me line near the top**, an "Included Versions" list (LED tea-light version / Bambu LED Kit 001 version / multi-colour sign option), "use the included 3MF" guidance, recommended filaments (Matte PLA called out by name), and a closing licence funnel: "Personal use only. To sell printed versions or access exclusive collections and early releases, join my Patreon." Source: https://makerworld.com/en/models/1980551-santa-s-workshop-light-up-house
- @P3DT's listings already do most of this (profiles, Handy path, Patreon licence line — see A2). The deltas worth copying: (1) the polite Boost line, (2) an explicit LED Kit 001 / tea-light *version* framing where a design supports it, (3) sibling cross-links + collection link in every description (B1).

#### D3. Covers, titles, tags — the discoverability stack
- Cover: "A real print photo as the cover [out-pulls a render]… Lead with a photo of a real print." Also: clear searchable title ("Name the object, not the project"), honest settings up front, and showing up afterwards ("Answering comments and adding photos of later prints keeps a listing alive"). Source: https://butteryspace.com/guides/upload-stl-to-makerworld/
- Cover craft from the Surrey Homeware creator guide: the cover "needs to immediately communicate what the item is and why someone would want it"; animated GIF covers work **only if the first frame works as a standalone picture**; shoot on a clean table near a sunny window; print the hero in a filament that photographs attractively. Source: https://makerworld.com/en/@surrey_homeware
- Forum guidance: "cover image is important, because that's what brings in views"; "use as many relevant 'tags' as possible. They impact your model's appearance in user's search"; use SEO-friendly titles (occasion + object, e.g. 'Valentine's Day Rose Gift Box'); enter contests *on theme* — off-theme entries risk removal. Sources: https://forum.bambulab.com/t/how-to-get-more-publicity/103927 and https://forum.bambulab.com/t/how-can-i-increase-my-model-popularity/143523?page=2
- For @P3DT specifically, the searchable phrases are the ones Scott already uses socially: "Christmas village," "tea light house," "light-up house," "no supports," "LED tea light." Titles should carry object + occasion + light function (e.g. "Townhouse — Christmas Village Tea Light House, No Supports"), not just the building's name.

#### D4. Small, quick, reliable prints out-download big complex ones — the village's entry pieces matter
- Creator-guide consensus: "Small items that print quickly and use little filament are always more popular than big, complex items" and "Items that print reliably will get better ratings and more promotion than items that are pushing the limits of the printer." Source: https://makerworld.com/en/@surrey_homeware
- Peer behaviour matches: NoOrdinaryLeaf (11.7k followers) releases tea-light shades in *pairs/sets with size variants* and open backs for a standard LED tea light, posting each as its own model + community post. Source: https://makerworld.com/en/@noordinaryleaf/posts
- Implication: lead each release week with the smallest/fastest-printing village piece available (accessory, fence/tree/stand, smallest house) as the download magnet, and let it pull traffic to the bigger houses via the collection + cross-links — rather than leading with the largest building.

#### D5. Community posts are a sanctioned traffic channel — with rules
- MakerWorld's community guidance encourages creator posts that document the design journey (pre-release previews, process, tips) and lists what *not* to do: mere link-reposting with little description, meaningless rating-sync posts, and boost/follow begging or trading. Source: https://makerworld.com/en/community/post/283758
- That is exactly the format the Click Into Celebration Spotlight Award rewards (A1) — one good process post per launch serves both the contest lane and the standing community lane.

#### D6. Maker's Supply / LED kit angle (flagged, not yet a recommendation to build)
- A social tips video lists "adding Maker's Supply parts and filaments to your bill of materials" as a points-earnings tip, and peer listings (D2) offer a Bambu LED Kit 001 version. I did not open a MakerWorld page quantifying Maker's Supply commission thresholds in this run, so any commission claim is **unverified** (see below). The *listing-copy* version of this (name the tea light / LED kit the model fits) is already covered in D2. Source for the tip's existence: https://www.facebook.com/reel/1649815599920346/
