Do not pay for Plus/Pro tiers at current (zero) volume; Free is the correct tier until ~$967/mo.

### A2. Meta Shop checkout — what is actually possible now

- **In-app Meta checkout no longer exists for Shops.** As of **September 2025, Shops on Facebook and Instagram use website checkout only**: shoppers browse / tap product tags on Meta, then complete purchase on the seller's own website. Meta discontinued Commerce Manager payouts, order management, returns and disputes with that change. Sources (secondary reporting quoting Meta Business Help Center, read 2026-11-13; **Meta's own help page was not directly opened in this pass — treat wording as well-corroborated but UNVERIFIED at first hand**): https://outfeed.ai/blog/facebook-commerce-manager/ , https://www.wittelsbach.ai/blog/do-facebook-shops-still-have-checkout/ , https://withnation.ai/guides/how-to-sell-on-instagram
- **A checkout URL is mandatory: without it the shop is not visible.** Same sources above. This exactly matches the known state of the OnBrandCraftz Meta Shop (unpublished, missing checkout URL) — there is no "turn on Meta checkout" fix; the missing piece is an external checkout destination.
- **Therefore the realistic route is:** Meta Shop (catalogue / discovery) → checkout URL → **Payhip product/store pages** (or Etsy product pages once Etsy reopens). Payhip is the only candidate destination that can exist *while Etsy is down*. Digital products' eligibility for Meta catalogues is restricted in some Meta guidance ("physical products you ship; services and digital products generally aren't eligible" — Delivered Social summary, https://deliveredsocial.com/social-commerce/ ) — **UNVERIFIED for OnBrandCraftz's specific catalogue; flag: Meta Shop may work for physical prints and refuse / limit digital-file listings. Verify in Commerce Manager before promising it.**
- **Effort/risk note:** Shop review can take time (secondary sources cite up to 4 weeks — **UNVERIFIED**). Do not count Meta Shop revenue before Christmas; treat any November setup as January infrastructure that also gives product tagging in December posts *if* it clears.

### A3. Direct sales via PayPal.me (+ Venmo) — what's realistic before Christmas

- **What it is:** a single public payment link; buyer enters an amount (amount can be pre-filled by appending it to the link — PayPal's own example pattern reported in secondary sources; **UNVERIFIED by opening PayPal's official PayPal.me help page in this pass**). There is no cart, no product page, no automatic file delivery, no inventory, no shipping calculation.
- **Fees — UNVERIFIED at first hand.** Secondary sources opened/searched 2026-11-13 disagree by route: "send/receive money for goods and services" reported at **2.99% (no fixed fee)** vs PayPal Checkout at **3.49% + $0.49**: https://legalclarity.org/who-pays-paypal-goods-and-services-fees-buyer-or-seller/ , https://instantdm.com/blog/accept-payments-without-website-creators-2026 . **Do not price on these numbers until Scott/Benson checks PayPal's official merchant fees page in-account.**
- **Protection caveat (secondary sources above):** Goods-and-Services payments carry PayPal Purchase Protection for buyers and conditions for seller protection (ship to the transaction address, keep tracking). Friends-and-Family carries no buyer protection and is not for sales. Digital-file delivery is hard to prove in a dispute — another reason PayPal.me suits **physical prints** (tracked shipping) better than files.
- **Realistic December role — yes, narrowly:**
  - ✅ One-to-one physical-print sales already happening in Messenger/DMs: agree item + shipping in chat, send the PayPal.me link, ship tracked, keep the tracking number with the order record. (Meta has no payout account attached — Messenger "Create order" is a record only, per memory.)
  - ✅ A "DM to order" line on physical-print posts/Stories while Etsy is down, with the Dec 12 cutoff stated.
  - ❌ Not a storefront: no discovery, no automated digital delivery (files would be emailed/messaged by hand — slow, error-prone, no download limits), no licence enforcement. Do not point public CTAs for *files* at PayPal.me; that's Payhip's job.
  - ⚠️ Public-page friction already known: the PayPal.me page displays "Scott Jackson", Greenfield IN — acceptable for the Support/Tip link (Scott's prior choice), but expect some buyers to hesitate at a personal name on a business purchase. The Payhip storefront (brand name, product pages, receipts) is the trust fix.
  - Venmo @Scott-Jackson-355 stays exactly as scoped: one-to-one only, never public pages (shows real name/photo).
