## Lane 4 — Revenue while Etsy is down + December

Full detail: `lane4-revenue-december.md`. Sources opened Nov 13: Gumroad pricing + official fees help; Payhip pricing, features, and billing help; B&H product pages for the four verified media prices.

- **Gumroad vs Payhip — verdict: Payhip Free Forever.** Verified: Payhip Free = 5% per sale, $0/mo, payout to your processor with no $100 minimum. Gumroad = 10% + $0.50 PLUS 2.9% + $0.30 processing, 30% on Discover sales, $100 payout minimum with a 7-day hold. On a $15 file, Payhip Free keeps ~$1.25 more per sale. Gumroad's only edge is Discover, unproven for STL buyers. Payhip also offers expiring download links + download limits, and its storefront can double as the Meta Shop checkout destination. Neither platform creates demand — both are checkout rails for traffic OBC already generates. Free tier is correct until ~$967/mo in sales.
- **PayPal.me:** realistic only for one-to-one PHYSICAL print sales via DM (tracked shipping). No cart, no auto file delivery — not a file storefront. Its fee rate is UNVERIFIED (secondary sources conflict: 2.99% vs 3.49% + $0.49; PayPal's official fees page was not opened).
