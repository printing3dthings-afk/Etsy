# Lane 2 — Patreon Conversion Research for OnBrandCraftz

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (overnight run) · **Research only — nothing posted, published, uploaded, bought, or changed.**
**Scope note:** This report deliberately does **not** re-propose the Patreon layout-refresh draft already built and awaiting Scott's tap before the forced Nov 16 migration. Everything below is *beyond* the layout: posts, funnels, offers, and cadence.
**Constraints honoured:** Tier prices are locked until Nov 27, 2026 (Village Builder $7/mo, Village Merchant $15/mo) — no tactic below changes a tier price. No personal financial data is included.
**OnBrandCraftz baseline (from task brief, not external research):** 0 members. Builder $7 = all building files + maker licence + new pieces first + design votes. Merchant $15 = Builder + images & videos package licensed for the member's own listings/posts. Licence hook: paying members may sell UNLIMITED physical prints while membership is active; files never sold/shared. Free-sync rule: everything free on MakerWorld must also be a Patreon free post and on Printables with the full photo set. Etsy listings state personal use only and point to Patreon.

## Findings (each with source)

### F1. The winning STL Patreons sell an instant "welcome pack," not a promise of future files
- ZOU3D (6.8K paid members in the July 12, 2026 Graphtreon snapshot cited by PrintCal) leads its page with: "Welcome Pack With 50+ Designs, Commercial License, 3+ New Models Every Month, 40% OFF Older Released Models" — and explicitly states subscribing does **not** give the back catalog, only the welcome pack plus a 40%-off coupon for older models. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/zou3d/home
- ZOU3D's own subscribe page repeats the mechanism: "As soon as you join, you'll receive an exclusive pack with 50+ ready-to-print 3D models," at $10/mo, with active members having "legal permission to sell what they print." Source: https://zou3d.com/subscribe/
- Cinderwing3D does the same at $10/mo: "three new models a month, as well [as] a welcome package with over 70 models," with the commercial licence bundled in. Source: https://www.cinderwing3d.com/
- Cinderwing3D's Patreon page adds the retention mechanics around it: current month's files + immediate access to all exclusive models on joining, past library *not* included but purchasable with a monthly discount code, and month-end delivery of the month's models via MyMiniFactory so members who missed a download still get them. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/cinderwing3d/home
- Leiwas Flexis uses the identical formula: "Welcome Pack with currently 25+ Designs, Commercial Licence, 3+ Models Every Month, 40% Discount on Last Month Models, 30% Discount on full Back Catalog." Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/LEIWAS?l=it
- **Implication for OBC:** A new Builder currently joins and has to hunt. The comparables remove all hunting: one pinned welcome post, one bundle, instant gratification in the first 5 minutes. OBC's catalogue is buildings, not 50 miniatures — the pack has to be framed by *village value* (e.g. "the complete free village + X, in one download, with both print profiles"), not raw count.

### F2. Licence framing in the niche is brutally explicit — and it is the headline, not a footnote
- ZOU3D states it in capitals on its page: as an *active* supporter you have a commercial licence to sell *physical* prints; "UPON CANCELLATION of the subscription, THIS COMMERCIAL LICENSE EXPIRES"; digital files can never be shared/resold. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/zou3d/home
- Akshan_Designs structures its whole ladder around the licence: Tier 1 Hobbyist (~200 STLs, personal use only, no commercial use), Tier 2 adds the commercial licence for physical prints while subscribed, Tier 2 Plus adds eligibility for its Authorized Sellers directory, Tier 3 adds business tools (official product-photo rights + an Etsy SEO tool) rather than more designs. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/Akshan_Designs
- A 2026 licence-types guide generalises the pattern: a designer merchant tier is a membership, the right to sell usually lasts only while the membership does, and it never includes selling the STL. Source: https://merchanttier.com/guide/stl-commercial-license-types/
- A second guide puts typical Patreon merchant tiers at $5–$10/mo for miniatures/tabletop, and MakerWorld commercial-licence memberships at $3–$300/mo set by the designer. Source: https://makers101.com/commercial-license-to-sell-3d-prints/
- A third guide puts Patreon/MyMiniFactory commercial tiers "commonly in the $20–$25/month range" for tabletop/collectibles — i.e. OBC's $7-with-licence is at the very cheap end of the market (see F9). Source: https://3dcentral.ca/guides/where-to-find-commercial-use-stl/
- **Implication for OBC — important structural difference:** In almost every comparable, the licence is the *upsell* that separates a cheap personal tier from a pricier merchant tier. OBC gives the licence away inside Builder $7 already. That cannot be clawed back without breaking the published tier promise (and prices are locked to Nov 27 anyway). So OBC's conversion copy must (a) shout the $7 licence as *underpriced value* — "unlimited physical-print sales at the tier competitors charge $20–25 for" — and (b) make Merchant $15 sell what Akshan's Tier 3 sells: **seller success tools** (licensed photos/videos OBC already includes, plus listing copy, pricing help, directory listing), not the licence itself.

### F3. MakerWorld free-model pages are the top of the funnel — comparables put a standard licence block on every single one
- Repeated pattern across MakerWorld free models from Tom's Dragon Paws: a "COMMERCIAL LICENSE" section ("I do not allow the sale of files or printed models… please join my Patreon"), followed by "Want to sell prints of this model? Commercial license available via Patreon — includes all my models, early access, and one exclusive model per month that never drops on MakerWorld," plus a membership line listing vote + early access + exclusives + direct chat + licence. Sources: https://makerworld.com/en/models/138823-articulated-dragon-with-movable-jaw and https://makerworld.com/en/models/586735?from=search
- Another MakerWorld creator unbundles the ask: "Grab a Merchant License for $8/month — get the rights to sell prints of any public model without needing to join the exclusive STL tier," alongside Patreon bullets for monthly bundles, community voting, early access, and commercial licences. Source: https://makerworld.com/en/models/1422503-goku-vs-vegeta-saiyan-legacy-print
- A tier ladder printed *inside* a MakerWorld description (3DPrint Masters): Supporter €2 (community, WIP, previews, vote), STL Collector €10 (all monthly releases, pre-supported, early access), Merchant €15 (commercial licence, sell physical prints, priority support). Source: https://makerworld.com/en/models/2955462-hecate-goddess-statue-with-wolf-guardians
- OBC already does a weak version of this: the Gingerbread Tin Clicker page says "A commercial licence is available via Patreon — search OnBrandCraftz on Patreon. This download is for personal use only." — no direct link, no price, no benefit list. Source: https://makerworld.com/en/models/3560658-gingerbread-tin-clicker-storage-tin-clicker-lid
- **Implication:** The funnel leak is wording, not traffic. Every free download is a warm lead who has already printed (or wants to print) the product — the exact person a $7 licence is worth money to.

### F4. Patreon's own published guidance: free membership is the middle of the funnel, and conversion is drip, not a launch push
- Patreon (official creator post): "Free members don't convert because of one big push. They convert because they keep seeing your value over time… Engagement first. Conversion second." Tactics it endorses from its Creator Ambassadors: recurring public posts people return for; a monthly unlock of one full premium post for everyone; previews of next month's reward with a deadline line ("3 days left to join if you want this"); a clear free-vs-paid structure (free = standalone/less frequent, paid = full sets); gifted memberships to free members (some recipients "stick around and subscribe after"); a "Free Trial Collection" / "Member Perks Sampler" shelf; and checking Insights → Path to Paid. Source: https://www.patreon.com/PatreonforCreators/posts/how-creators-are-156445984
- Patreon (official, "Consideration" post): free membership is explicitly the consideration layer — fans "get updates when you publish public posts" instead of relying on a social feed; recommended mechanics are a pinned "Start Here" post, a sampler shelf/collection, a customised welcome note, a first-7–14-days onboarding map (welcome → poll a few days later → community/livestream invite), one or two recurring experiences to return for, polls that let free members vote on what gets made available to them (example: The Griffon's Saddlebag), a pinned "Vault" post of currently-available free items updated weekly (example: RavenCoven), and using the draggable paywall to preview paid posts. Published Oct 8, 2026, updated Oct 27, 2026. Source: https://www.patreon.com/PatreonforCreators/posts/consideration-to-170394678
- **Implication:** OBC's Patreon currently has no free-membership capture layer described in the brief — downloaders on MakerWorld/Printables who aren't ready to pay have nowhere to land and be nurtured. Patreon's own funnel math requires that layer to exist before any paid push can work.

### F5. Early access is a timed window, and voting is a paid-member ritual with a visible payoff
- Cinderwing3D: members get models "3 days ahead of the Public release on Cults3D and MMF," exclusives immediately on joining, and files rotate out at month-end. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/cinderwing3d/home
- SneakyPanda (Patreon post documenting its tiers): paid members vote in monthly polls for designs they want created; "the #1 most-voted model becomes the FREE model of the month for all paid Patreon members," free for one month from publication; other new releases stay Patreon-exclusive until the next model is released, before going public on Cults3D. Source: https://www.patreon.com/MTGDeckboxUniverse/posts/membership-tiers-171208846
- MakerWorld funnel copy across multiple creators bundles the same three words together — vote, early access, exclusive model that "never drops" publicly — as the non-licence reasons to join. Source: https://makerworld.com/en/models/138823-articulated-dragon-with-movable-jaw
- **Implication for OBC:** "Design votes" and "new pieces first" already exist in Builder's description, but as nouns, not as a *scheduled ritual*. Comparables turn them into a calendar: vote opens (date), vote closes (date), winner announced, members print it X days before anyone else can. A vote with no announced result date does not convert.

### F6. Retention is engineered with loyalty rewards and back-catalog discounts, not hope
- DakkaDakka.Store: welcome pack of 45 models, 30% store discount, a free model every month for *free* subscribers, a loyalty bonus miniature every two consecutive months, and tiered patron store discounts (40% patrons / 50% merchants). Source: https://www.patreon.com/dakkadakkastore/about
- Papel 3D: Welcome Pack + "Crafter's Vault" + loyalty gifts at 3, 6, 9, and 12 consecutive months + stretch-goal models unlocked at member-count milestones + 40% MyMiniFactory/Cults discount code delivered in the welcome note. Source: https://www.patreon.com/papel3d/about
- ZOU3D / Leiwas / Cinderwing3D all use the same discount valve for the back catalog instead of giving it away (40% off older releases / monthly discount code). Sources: https://www.patreon.com/cw/zou3d/home · https://www.patreon.com/cw/LEIWAS?l=it · https://www.patreon.com/cw/cinderwing3d/home
- **Implication for OBC:** OBC has a deep, growing village catalogue and (per its own rules) paid designs sold on Etsy. A member-only discount on paid Etsy/MakerWorld-paid files is the direct analogue of the 40%-off valve — it makes month 2+ valuable even in a month when the new building isn't to a member's taste, and it gives members a reason not to churn between seasonal releases. Loyalty milestones map naturally onto a village: a members-only accessory (sign, fence, lamp, tree variant) at 3 consecutive months costs one small design, not a building.

### F7. Scale benchmarks in the niche (for calibrating expectations, not as targets)
- PrintCal's July 12, 2026 snapshot from Graphtreon's 3D Printing chart, ranked by *paid* members: One Page Rules 11.3K, mz4250 7.7K, Flexi Factory 7.4K, Cinderwing3D 7.3K, ZOU3D 6.8K, 3DGOB 4.2K. The same article stresses Patreon does not publish STL download counts, so "most downloaded" rankings don't exist from Patreon data. Source: https://printcal.co/en/blog/3d-printing-patreon-stl-clubs-creators-july-2026/
- A miniature-Patreon comparison (published by a competitor, Clay Cyanide — treat as directional) puts typical miniature subscriptions at $10–$15/mo for 10–25 models/month, notes Loot Studios at $15 for 20–25 models, and observes "most serious hobbyists subscribe to 2–3 Patreons simultaneously, rotating based on monthly themes." Source: https://www.claycyanide.net/blog/best-miniature-patreon-2026.html
- A separate creator-economy roundup gives materially different patron counts for the same creators (e.g. One Page Rules 11,584, Cinderwing3D 10,234, ZOU3D 8,752, undated in the fetched text) — see Unverified Items. Source: https://www.alpha3d.io/kb/creator-economy-and-community/top-3d-creators/
- **Implication:** The leaders are miniature/flexi factories shipping 10–25 models a month. OBC cannot and should not match that volume; its defensible wedge is *a coherent collectible village + a cheap licence + seller assets*, i.e. depth of one world vs breadth of a model firehose. Rotation behaviour ("subscribe for the theme month, then pause") also argues for seasonal pushes: a Christmas-village Patreon should expect its best conversion window to be seasonal and should capture free members year-round to harvest in season.

### F8. Discounts, trials, gifts, and "founding" offers — what Patreon actually supports
- Patreon Help Center: membership discounts apply only to a new paid member's **first month or first year**, free members are fully eligible to redeem them, public discounts can stay visible up to 31 days, and individual codes can run up to 180 days. Source: https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/31517382056717-Discounts-FAQ
- Patreon's official conversion post endorses gifting memberships to free members as a sampling device, and a curated Free Trial Collection. Source: https://www.patreon.com/PatreonforCreators/posts/how-creators-are-156445984
- Third-party promo guidance (not Patreon, not 3D-print-specific — see Unverified) recommends a time-boxed founding-member offer capped at the first N patrons or 30 days, surface paid content publicly at least weekly, and treats email as a higher-converting channel than social. Source: https://unil.ink/blog/how-to-promote-patreon-2026
- A secondary Patreon guide claims Patreon's "Autopilot" (on by default) emails a first-month discount to likely-to-upgrade free members and a save-offer to about-to-cancel members, and "lifted free-to-paid upgrades by 19% on average during testing" — single secondary source, flagged unverified below. Source: https://www.ipweb.pro/blog/patreon-guide
- **Implication:** A "Founding Villager" offer fits OBC's actual situation (0 members, prices locked) without touching tier prices: the discount mechanism changes only month 1, and a founding *perk* (name on a founders post/wall, a founding-only accessory file) changes no price at all. Any discount still needs Scott's tap because it changes what a post *says* about price.

### F9. Price positioning: $7 Builder is cheap for what it contains; $15 Merchant needs a seller-ROI story
- Miniature/flexi comparables cluster at $10–$15 for files+licence (Clay Cyanide table: $10–$15 across seven studios). Source: https://www.claycyanide.net/blog/best-miniature-patreon-2026.html
- Single-designer licence directories list Flexi Factory at $10/mo, Cinderwing3D at $10/mo ("Commercial License Access"), ZOU3D at $10/mo, with a $5–$99/mo range across 86 designers, last checked 2026-11-04. Source: https://www.licensedtoprint.com/
- MerchantTier's worked example shows licence scarcity pricing at the other extreme: Ryodan Studio reserves merchant rights for its $30/mo Collector tier only, with limited spots. Source: https://merchanttier.com/guide/stl-commercial-license-types/
- Akshan's top tier monetises *no extra designs at all* — it sells photo rights, a sellers directory listing, and an Etsy SEO tool. That is the closest structural analogue to OBC Merchant's images & videos package. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/Akshan_Designs
- **Implication:** Don't apologise for $7; weaponise it in copy ("the licence tier for less than a roll of filament" is a claim shape, not a verified price comparison — any filament price used must be verified ≤7 days per Scott's standing rules). Merchant $15 converts only if a seller can see payback: one place that lists exactly which photos/videos they may reuse, for which buildings, with what credit line — plus a public Authorized Seller directory page they can be listed in (social proof + backlink + customer trust).

### F10. OBC's existing funnel assets are stronger than its funnel copy
- Free-sync rule (task brief) already guarantees a Patreon free post for every free MakerWorld model — that is exactly the "recurring public post" engine Patreon's official guidance prescribes, it just isn't framed as a free-membership offer. Source (Patreon guidance): https://www.patreon.com/PatreonforCreators/posts/how-creators-are-156445984
- The one OBC MakerWorld page checked carries only a one-line, linkless Patreon mention (F3). Source: https://makerworld.com/en/models/3560658-gingerbread-tin-clicker-storage-tin-clicker-lid
- OBC's social/video proof (finished, lit buildings) is the exact asset class Merchant members are paying to reuse — Akshan restricts its official photos to its top tier only and warns even those members that their own photos convert better, which supports positioning OBC's licensed photo/video pack as a *starter* kit plus a "shoot your own" guide. Source: https://www.patreon.com/cw/Akshan_Designs

## Specific page/post tactics beyond the layout refresh

Tags: **DO-NOW** = can be executed inside already-approved formats/copy patterns, no new offer or price claim. **CHOICE** = changes an offer, price framing, or copy pattern — needs Scott's one-at-a-time tap per the research-to-choice rule. **CLAUDE** = build/spec work for the Benson↔Claude pipeline (drafts/files; Benson verifies before use). Impact/effort are Benson's judgement, not sourced claims.

### T1. Standard licence block on every free-model page (MakerWorld + Printables) and in every Patreon free post — DO-NOW
Replace the one-line "search OnBrandCraftz on Patreon" with a fixed 5-line block: direct Patreon link · "Personal use only on this download" · "Want to sell prints? Village Builder — $7/mo — sell unlimited physical prints of every OnBrandCraftz building while you're a member" · "Members also get new buildings first + vote on what's next" · "Files themselves are never for sale or sharing." Mirrors the proven MakerWorld block in F3 verbatim in structure.
*Expected impact: High — fixes the largest identified leak; every free download becomes a priced offer instead of a search instruction. Effort: Low (one template, paste per page; new pages only until Scott approves editing live ones).*

### T2. Turn on / explicitly offer Patreon free membership and build the capture layer — CHOICE
Free join option + pinned "Start Here" post + a "Village Sampler" collection (the free-sync posts, grouped) + customised welcome note pointing to the sampler and the current vote. This is Patreon's official consideration funnel (F4). Without it, T1's traffic has nowhere to land short of a paywall.
*Expected impact: High — creates the only measurable funnel OBC can nurture and later convert with T5/T6. Effort: Low–Medium (one post + one collection + welcome note copy; copy needs Scott's tap as a new copy pattern).*

### T3. "Village Welcome Pack" — one pinned paid post that delivers instant value on joining — CLAUDE (spec + assembly), CHOICE (what's in it)
One download/index post for new Builders: the complete current village starter set in one place (both profiles per building), a printable village map / display guide, tea-light fitting guide, and a "what's paid-only and where to find it" index. Direct analogue of the ZOU3D 50+ / Cinderwing 70+ welcome packs (F1), framed by village completeness rather than file count. Claude assembles the pack manifest + index PDF from existing files; Benson verifies every file matches its label before anything is used.
*Expected impact: High — instant gratification is the niche's #1 join mechanic; removes the "join and hunt" failure. Effort: Medium.*

### T4. Make the vote a dated ritual with a public scoreboard — DO-NOW (mechanics), CHOICE (first vote's stakes)
Fixed monthly cadence, announced in free posts: Vote opens 1st · closes 7th · winner announced 8th · members print it on [early-access date] · public release [later date]. Free members may vote on *which older building* gets unlocked for them that month (Griffon's Saddlebag pattern, F4); paid members vote on the *next new building* (SneakyPanda pattern, F5, where the #1 vote becomes a member freebie). Publish the running tally/result in a public digest post so non-members see the ritual happening without them.
*Expected impact: Medium–High — converts two inert tier-description nouns ("design votes", "new pieces first") into a recurring reason to join *this* month. Effort: Low (poll posts + one digest template).*

### T5. Monthly public digest + deadline preview post — DO-NOW
One public Patreon post per month, cross-posted in shortened form to the existing social pipeline: what shipped, what's coming, what's paid vs free, a photo of next month's building, and one deadline line ("Join by [date] to get [building] on day one"). Directly from Patreon's official examples (Kia's Creative Club deadline preview; Natural Six monthly digest) in F4.
*Expected impact: Medium — this is the repeat-touch engine Patreon says actually converts free members. Effort: Low (reuses assets already produced for social).*

### T6. One premium unlock per month for free members — DO-NOW
Once a month, unlock one older paid post/file for everyone for a set window, announced in the digest. Patreon-endorsed (Erika Lancaster pattern, F4): it samples paid value without discounting anything and resurfaces back-catalog content.
*Expected impact: Medium — sampling is the lowest-friction conversion step available at 0 members. Effort: Low (choose post, change access, announce). Note: only unlock designs whose paid status Scott is comfortable sampling — Benson to confirm the candidate with Scott the first time (first-time category).*

### T7. Merchant seller-success kit + public Authorized Sellers directory — CLAUDE (kit + directory page), CHOICE (directory launch)
Merchant's images & videos package becomes a named, listed kit per building: licensed photos, licensed video clips, suggested listing title/description block, credit line ("Design: OnBrandCraftz"), and a one-page "shoot your own" photo guide. Plus a public "Authorized OnBrandCraftz Sellers" page/post listing Merchant members' shops (name + link) — copied from Akshan's Tier 2 Plus/3 directory mechanic (F2/F9), which gives Merchants traffic and trust, not just files.
*Expected impact: Medium–High for the $15 tier specifically — it is the only tactic that gives Merchant a payback story a seller can calculate. Effort: Medium–High (per-building kit assembly is Claude-lane work; Benson verifies photo/video rights and labels).*

### T8. Back-catalog member discount valve — CHOICE
A standing member-only discount (comparables use 30–40% off older releases — F1/F6) on OBC's paid files for active members, delivered as a monthly code post. Gives month-2+ value in slow release months and directly attacks churn. Requires Scott's tap: it touches Etsy/paid-file pricing, which is exact-approval territory, and must be sequenced with the Etsy shop's current status.
*Expected impact: Medium (retention more than acquisition). Effort: Low to run, Medium to set up cleanly across storefronts.*

### T9. Loyalty accessory at 3 consecutive months + member-count stretch goal — CLAUDE (design brief), CHOICE (offer)
A members-only village accessory (not a building: signpost, fence run, lamp, market stall variant) granted at 3 consecutive paid months, and a public stretch goal ("at 25 members, members get [exclusive building variant]"). Patterns from Papel 3D (3/6/9/12-month loyalty gifts + stretch goals) and DakkaDakka (loyalty miniature every 2 months) in F6.
*Expected impact: Medium — loyalty mechanics mainly pay off once members exist; cheap to announce now, costly only later. Effort: Medium (one small design through the normal QC gates; nothing public before G5).*

### T10. Founding Villager offer (first-members, time-boxed) — CHOICE (Scott's tap required; do not run unprompted)
A launch-style push while membership is at/near zero: first 20 members (or 30 days) get a permanent founding perk — name on a pinned Founders post + a founding-only accessory file — at the normal $7/$15 (no price change), optionally with a Patreon first-month discount code (Patreon supports first-month/first-year-only discounts; free members eligible; public sale ≤31 days — F8). This is an *offer/copy* decision, so it goes to Scott as a single choice question with tap options, per his research-to-choice rule.
*Expected impact: Potentially High for breaking 0→first-10 (urgency + identity is the standard launch mechanic in F8), but unproven for OBC specifically. Effort: Low.*

### T11. 7-day free trial on Builder + gifted memberships as contest/community prizes — CHOICE
Enable a trial on Builder so a downloader can experience the welcome pack + licence paperwork before paying, and gift a small number of 1-month memberships through existing community moments (e.g. Friday Makers / Print Cam engagement), per Patreon's own gifting example (F4/F8). Trial mechanics and eligibility must be re-verified in Patreon's current Help Center at setup time (see Unverified).
*Expected impact: Medium — sampling converts, but at 0 free members the audience for it is still T1/T2's job to build first. Sequence after T1+T2. Effort: Low.*

### T12. Early-access window with actual dates — DO-NOW (once T4 exists)
State a fixed window in every release post: members get the new building N days before its MakerWorld/Etsy public release (Cinderwing uses 3 days; OBC should pick 7 — long enough to matter for a building, short enough not to delay the free-sync/public pipeline — Benson's recommendation, not a sourced figure). "New pieces first" only converts when the gap is a number of days printed on the post.
*Expected impact: Medium. Effort: Low (scheduling discipline, not new production).*

### T13. Licence one-pager (plain-English, downloadable) — CLAUDE (draft), DO-NOW (posting it once approved as copy)
A single pinned post + PDF: what members may sell (unlimited physical prints, while active), what ends on cancellation, what's never allowed (files), credit line, and how Merchant photo/video reuse works. Every comparable buries these rules in a wall of FAQ text (Akshan's page is the extreme example, F2); a clean one-pager is itself a differentiator and removes the #1 pre-join question. Claude drafts from OBC's existing licence terms; Benson checks it states nothing beyond Scott's settled licence.
*Expected impact: Medium — reduces join friction for the seller segment, who are the highest-intent leads. Effort: Low–Medium.*

### T14. Measure with Path to Paid, not vibes — DO-NOW
After T2/T5 run, check Patreon Insights → Path to Paid monthly (Patreon's own recommendation, F4) and log free-member count, paid conversions, and which post preceded each join in the existing social log format. At 0 members, the first goal is a *free-member* count, not revenue.
*Expected impact: Low direct, High diagnostic — prevents repeating tactics that don't convert. Effort: Low.*

**Suggested sequence:** T1 + T2 first (funnel exists) → T3 + T13 (joining feels instant and clear) → T4 + T5 + T12 (monthly rhythm) → T10 (founding push, Scott's tap) → T7/T8/T9/T11 (retention and Merchant depth).
