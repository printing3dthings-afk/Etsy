### Proposal 1 — BEST: Fix the carousel type floor — body ≥40px, cut words instead of shrinking type (from Wed Nov 11)
- **Specific change:** Rebuild the two text-led templates to a measured floor: body text ≥40px on a 1080px canvas, headlines ≥64px, and fix Explained's burnt-orange small-label contrast (measured 2.83:1 on cream — below even the 3:1 large-text threshold). News & Hype currently builds body at 24–25px. Where copy doesn't fit at the floor, the copy gets cut, not the type shrunk. Add the display-crop preview to platform-fit QC at the same time (Maker's Blueprint is built 9:16, a ratio Instagram's feed does not display full-frame — dual-export it).
- **Re-checked this morning:** the arithmetic holds across independent sources — on a 360px-wide phone, 40px canvas text displays at ~13.3px, above the 12px mobile legibility floor; 24px displays at 8.0px, below it on every common phone. Sources: https://pineable.com/blog/social-media-carousel-design-best-practices and https://medium.com/@frankheijdenrijk/how-to-make-instagram-carousels-people-save-covers-slide-2-type-sizes-and-2026-specs-d79ec7f54906
- **Why it wins the week:** it is the only proposal that is a *measured defect in work already shipping*, in a series Scott already flagged once (News & Hype, Nov 3). It is not a taste experiment and needs no read horizon to justify.
- **Which series it lifts:** News & Hype first, then Explained and Maker's Blueprint. **Effort:** Low–medium (template rebuild, no identity change — same colours, fonts, grids, just legible sizes).

### Proposal 3 — Arachne A/B on ONE thin-pane house — slice preview first, print second (from Sat Nov 14)
- **Specific change:** Take one thin-pane house (panes in our standard 0.8–1.0mm band) and slice it twice — stock Classic vs Arachne wall generator — and compare the previews layer by layer for dropped/gap-filled pane features before anything prints. Only if the preview shows a real difference does a single test print follow; any profile talk needs a second, separate approval. Arachne is not adopted as a default on the strength of a preview alone.
- **Re-checked this morning:** Bambu's own wiki confirms Arachne is a Quality → Wall generator option in Bambu Studio with known trade-offs (seam, floating perimeters, uneven surfaces are the documented cases for switching back to Classic), and slicer documentation confirms Arachne varies line width to print thin features Classic would skip, with the effect concentrated in the 1–3 nozzle-width band — exactly our pane band. Sources: https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/software/bambu-studio/WallGenerator and https://orcaslicer.org/wall-generator-settings/
- **Why it survives synthesis:** it is the week's only proposal that improves the *product* (print fidelity of the panes every glowing house depends on), not just its presentation — Scott's Nov 4 directive covers design as well as content. Its companion finding (purge waste is counted in layers, not colours — Sat P2) stays as a strong runner-up for an Explained/Versus pair once this test gives us our own sliced numbers to teach from.
- **Which work it lifts:** print quality for the whole village line; feeds Explained and Versus with an OUR RESULT if it prints. **Effort:** Low for the preview stage (desk work only); one small print only on a preview win.
