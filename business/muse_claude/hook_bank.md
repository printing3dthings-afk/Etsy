# Hook bank: the opening lines of our videos and posts

Started 2026-11-25 (Scott: "Just seeing what we can use", from an Instagram post on AI content
prompts). One list of the first line a viewer sees or hears, so each new video starts from hooks
that worked, not from scratch.

**How it works:**
- **Claude** adds a row for every video delivered to `video_queue/`. The hook is the first
  on-screen line, or the voice-over's first line if that comes first.
- **Benson** fills in the result from `data/social/muse_log.md` once a post has run a week: the
  platform, views, average watch time or retention if the platform shows it, saves and comments.
- A hook goes on the **Worked** list when its post beat the series' usual numbers, and on
  **Didn't** when it fell short. New videos borrow from Worked.
- Every hook still passes the usual claim check (STANDARDS 15): a hook makes a claim too.
