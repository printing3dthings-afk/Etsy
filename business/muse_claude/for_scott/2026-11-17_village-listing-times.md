**Watch out for these:**
- **Victorian Santa's Workshop has two versions.** v1 is 15 h 40 m; v2 (Scott approved the
  swap Nov 15) is 17 h 34 m. The listing must give the time of the file it actually sells.
  If the sale ZIP still holds v1, either swap the ZIP or quote 15 h 40 m. Don't quote v2's
  time for v1's file.
- **The two Santa's Workshops are the only long prints** (12–18 hours). Say so plainly in
  their listings; a buyer expecting "an evening" will be disappointed.
- **Four-colour times are much longer** (Workshop v2 four colour: 2 d 22 h). Listings that
  show colour photos should give both times, or say the time is for one colour.
