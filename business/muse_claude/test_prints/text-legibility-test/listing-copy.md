# TEST - Text Legibility Test

**Title (MakerWorld, <= 50 chars):** TEST - Text Legibility Test

**What it tests:** the smallest text your printer + slicer settings can
actually render - in the three forms text appears on real parts. One
build plate carries three 216 x 78 x 3 mm plates with identical rows:
nominal text heights 10 / 8 / 6 / 5 / 4 / 3 mm, each row the line
"<size> QUICK BROWN FOX 0123" (the size number leads its own row).

- **RAISED** - text standing 0.8 mm proud of the top face.
- **ENGRAVED** - text cut 0.8 mm into the top face.
- **FACE DOWN** - text cut into the BOTTOM face, mirrored, so it prints
  against the build plate and reads correctly when the plate is flipped
  over; the plate's own texture becomes the letter face.

**How to judge:** print one profile, then read down each plate's rows
under your normal bench light. The smallest row you can read without
squinting or magnification is your legibility floor for that form.
Expect the floor to differ per form - and expect face-down text on a
textured plate to behave differently from the same size engraved.

**Profiles in this pack (the profiles ARE the comparison):**

- Standard: 0.20 mm Standard - verified slice 3 h 15 min - 135.48 g.
- Optimized: 0.16 mm Optimal - verified slice 3 h 57 min - 154.59 g (the finer
  layer height is the variable most likely to move the floor on the
  small rows - print both and compare row by row).

**Filament:** matte white PLA, single colour. **Ironing: OFF** - ironing
would smooth the raised letters' tops and change what is being measured.
Supports off. The gallery images are renders of the exact print files
until a real print replaces them.
