# TEST — Bridging Span Test

Title: TEST — Bridging Span Test

## Description

Bridging is printing in mid-air between two points — and every setup has a
span where the strands stop staying flat and start to sag. This plate finds
that span for your printer, filament and cooling.

Two rows of posts stand on a base plate (158 x 66 mm). Bars 5 mm wide and
3 mm thick bridge the gaps: 10, 30 and 50 mm on one row, 20, 40 and 60 mm on
the other, each span engraved on the base in front of its gap.

### How to judge

Print it, let it cool, and look at each bridge from the side and below. A good
bridge is flat with tight, even strands; a failing one dips in the middle or
shows loose, separated strands. Measure the dip with calipers if you want
numbers. The longest span that stays flat is your design limit for lids,
windows and horizontal holes in that filament.

### Profiles and material

Both profiles are matte white PLA, 3 walls, supports off (supports would
defeat the test):

- Standard: 0.20 mm Standard - verified slice 50 min 42 sec - 36.04 g
- Optimized: 0.16 mm Optimal - verified slice 1 hr 07 min - 40.33 g

MakerWorld may display its own re-sliced time and filament estimates.

Prints the Bridging Span Test: two rows of posts on a 158 x 66 mm base with 5 x 3 mm bridge bars spanning 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 mm gaps, each span engraved on the base. Matte white PLA, 0.20 mm Standard, 3 walls, 15% infill, supports off. Verified slice: 50 min 42 sec - 36.04 g.

## Profile copy — Optimized

Prints the Bridging Span Test: two rows of posts on a 158 x 66 mm base with 5 x 3 mm bridge bars spanning 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 mm gaps, each span engraved on the base. Matte white PLA, 0.16 mm Optimal, 3 walls, 12% gyroid infill, supports off. Verified slice: 1 hr 07 min - 40.33 g. Finer layers give flatter, tighter bridge strands than the Standard profile.
