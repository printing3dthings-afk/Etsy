# TEST — First-Layer Adhesion Map

Title: TEST — First-Layer Adhesion Map

## Description

First-layer problems hide in zones: a corner that always lifts, a patch where
the plate is a touch low, a fingerprint spot that never sticks. This map print
shows you the whole plate at once.

One hundred squares, each 20 x 20 mm and just 0.6 mm thick, spread in a grid
across the printable area of the 256 mm plate. A thin rail runs along the
front edge and the front-left square has a hole in it, so you always know
which way the map came off the plate. (The grid starts beside the small
18 x 28 mm exclusion zone in the plate's front-left corner — that corner
cannot print on this machine.)

### How to judge

Print it on your usual first-layer settings and examine the map before flexing
the plate. Perfect zones: every square flat, fully stuck, same sheen and
thickness. Problem zones tell their story: a lifted corner means that area is
low or contaminated; a square that prints thin and rough means the nozzle is
closer there; a square that lets go mid-print marks an adhesion dead spot.
Sketch or photograph the grid and note the bad zones — then clean, re-level or
adjust and print it again to confirm the fix.

### Profiles and material

Both profiles are matte white PLA, supports off, no brim and no ironing —
anything that pins the squares down or presses them flat would hide what the
map measures:

- Standard: 0.20 mm Standard - verified slice 1 hr 11 min - 31.24 g (squares print at exactly 3 layers)
- Optimized: 0.16 mm Optimal - verified slice 1 hr 26 min - 35.51 g (squares print at 4 layers)

MakerWorld may display its own re-sliced time and filament estimates.

The gallery images are renders of the map until the print is photographed.

Prints the First-Layer Adhesion Map: a 10 x 10 grid of 20 x 20 x 0.6 mm squares across the printable area of the 256 mm plate, with a front-edge rail and a holed front-left square as orientation markers. Matte white PLA, 0.20 mm Standard, 3 walls, 15% infill, supports off, no brim. Verified slice: 1 hr 11 min - 31.24 g.

## Profile copy — Optimized

Prints the First-Layer Adhesion Map: a 10 x 10 grid of 20 x 20 x 0.6 mm squares across the printable area of the 256 mm plate, with a front-edge rail and a holed front-left square as orientation markers. Matte white PLA, 0.16 mm Optimal, 3 walls, 12% gyroid infill, supports off, no brim. Verified slice: 1 hr 26 min - 35.51 g. Squares print at 4 layers (0.64 mm) at this layer height.
