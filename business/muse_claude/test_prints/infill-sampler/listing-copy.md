# TEST — Infill Sampler

Title: TEST — Infill Sampler

## Description

Infill is the hidden structure inside almost every print — and the
percentage is usually picked by habit. This plate replaces habit with
evidence: seven identical 40 x 40 x 12 mm blocks, printed in one job, each
with a different infill. The tops are left open (no top shell) so you can
see and feel the actual pattern.

On the plate: gyroid at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 40%, grid at 15%, and lightning
at 15%. Each block is embossed with its setting on the front face.
Everything else is identical — 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, matte white PLA —
so any difference you measure is the infill.

### How to judge

Weigh each block, then test them: stand on them, squeeze them in a vice,
or flex them until one complains. Note where stiffness stops improving —
for most functional parts the jump from 15% to 40% costs far more time and
filament than the strength it buys, and lightning is only there to hold up
top surfaces, not to carry load.
