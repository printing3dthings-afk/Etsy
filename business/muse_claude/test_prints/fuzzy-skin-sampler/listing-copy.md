# TEST — Fuzzy Skin Sampler

Title: TEST — Fuzzy Skin Sampler

## Description

Fuzzy skin jitters the outer wall by a fraction of a millimetre as it
prints, trading a glossy layered look for a matte, cast texture that hides
layer lines completely. The question is how much jitter is enough — this
plate answers it by touch.

Four identical cups (46 mm across, 42 mm tall) print in one job with
per-object fuzzy skin settings: OFF, then thickness 0.06, 0.12 and
0.18 mm, all at a 0.12 mm point distance. Fuzzy applies to the outside
walls only, so every cup's inside is a smooth control surface. A smooth
label plate in front of each cup says which is which. Everything else is
identical — 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, matte white PLA.

### How to judge

Run a fingertip over each cup, then look at them side by side under a
window or lamp. Note where layer lines disappear, where the texture starts
to look like orange peel, and whether fine detail would survive. For most
decorative parts the middle settings do the hiding without the roughness.
