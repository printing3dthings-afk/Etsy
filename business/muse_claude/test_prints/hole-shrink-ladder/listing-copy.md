# TEST — Hole Shrink Ladder

Title: TEST — Hole Shrink Ladder

## Description

Printed holes almost never measure what the design says — they come out small,
and the shrink is not the same at every size. This ladder turns that guesswork
into numbers for your filament.

One plate (148 x 76 x 5 mm) carries through-holes at 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10 mm,
each engraved with its designed size, plus a matching row of solid pegs at the
same diameters so you can check outside sizes too.

### How to measure

Print it, let it cool completely, then test each hole with drill bits, pin
gauges or dowels: the largest one that slides in with light finger pressure is
the hole's real size. Measure the pegs with calipers. Write your results in the
table — the difference from the designed size is the compensation to design with.

### Profiles and material

Both profiles are matte white PLA, 3 walls, supports off, no ironing (an
ironing pass can lip a hole mouth, and the hole mouths are the measuring
surface here):

- Standard: 0.20 mm Standard - verified slice 49 min 49 sec - 36.96 g
- Optimized: 0.16 mm Optimal - verified slice 1 hr 07 min - 40.38 g (finer
  layers give rounder, cleaner bores)

MakerWorld may display its own re-sliced time and filament estimates.

The gallery images are renders of the plate until the print is photographed.

## Profile copy — Standard

Prints the Hole Shrink Ladder: a 148 x 76 x 5 mm plate with through-holes at 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10 mm and matching solid pegs at the same sizes, each labelled with its designed size. Matte white PLA, 0.20 mm Standard, 3 walls, 15% infill, supports off, no ironing. Verified slice: 49 min 49 sec - 36.96 g.

## Profile copy — Optimized

Prints the Hole Shrink Ladder: a 148 x 76 x 5 mm plate with through-holes at 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10 mm and matching solid pegs at the same sizes, each labelled with its designed size. Matte white PLA, 0.16 mm Optimal, 3 walls, 12% gyroid infill, supports off, no ironing. Verified slice: 1 hr 07 min - 40.38 g. The finer layers give rounder, cleaner hole bores than the Standard profile.
