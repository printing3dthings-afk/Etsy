  each hole was opened on its own. No physics claim beyond that is made on screen.
- **The order is rearranged.** Scott played closed, then 8 mm, then 5 mm; the lesson shows
  closed, 5 mm, 8 mm. Each card plays its own note's clip, so what you hear matches its number.
- **The whistle is a diagram,** labelled "Diagram of our test whistle". It's not a photo or a
  render of the print. A real photo of the whistle would be better: swap it in if Scott has one.
- **Pitch wanders with breath** (±20 cents within a note, `BRIEF.md`). Each number is the
