# Brand marks as true vectors — for 3D print inlays

Generated by `tools/text_to_svg.py` from the real font outlines. Not traces.
Regenerate with the exact commands at the bottom.

| file | face | ships at | **minimum printable width** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OBC.svg` | Montserrat Black | 20 × 6.29 mm | **9.4 mm** |
| `OnBrandCraftz.svg` | Caveat Bold | 80 × 12.11 mm | **50.5 mm** |

Both are one `<path>`, one fill, and extrude **watertight** — 1,012 and 5,704
facets, imported in 0.1s. They scale freely; the shipped size is a starting
point, the minimum is not.

## The minimum width is the number that matters

The standing rule is 2 extrusions (0.84mm) on the thinnest stroke. Below that
the slicer has less than one bead to work with and the stroke drops out — this
is the defect that printed a blank mark on three of four sauce models. Because
stroke-to-width is fixed for a given face, one measurement gives the minimum at
any size:

| mark | thinnest stroke ÷ width | width needed for 0.84mm |
|---|---|---|
| OBC, Montserrat Black | 0.0890 | 9.4 mm |
| OnBrandCraftz, Caveat Bold | 0.0166 | 50.5 mm |

**Under ~51mm wide, use OBC instead of the wordmark.** That is the whole reason
OBC exists.

## Why the real brush logo is not in here

The shop's actual logo (`tools/api_server/static/brand/onbrandcraftz-wordmark.svg`,
an SVG wrapper around a PNG; traced into `assets/brand_vector/`) **cannot print
as an inlay on a P1S at any size that fits the plate.** Measured off the source
bitmap with a distance transform, its hairline connectors are **2px on a 1188px
mark — 0.168% of the width**. For those to reach 0.84mm the logo would have to
be **499mm wide**; the plate is 256mm. Even its *median* stroke needs 138mm.

It stays what it is — a logo for listings and the dashboard. For anything that
gets printed, use the two files here.

## Using them in Bambu Studio

Drag the SVG in, set the extrude height, then Color Painting (**N**) → Fill to
assign the filament. `width`/`height` are in real mm with a matching viewBox, so
it imports at the size in the table rather than an arbitrary one.

## Fonts are not clean, and this tool cleans them

Montserrat's `B` is drawn as **two** contours, not three: one ring traces the
lower counter, steps up 19 units, traces the upper counter, then closes down a
line 5 units away — leaving a 0.042mm sliver through the B's middle bar.
FreeType hides it. OpenSCAD's SVG import tessellated it into a visible wedge and
the extrusion came out **non-manifold, 12 broken faces**. So outlines are
flattened, run through a real polygon boolean, and slots narrower than
`--heal-mm` (default 0.06) are closed. One extrusion is 0.42mm — a 0.042mm slot
could never print, so closing it is the honest reading, not a liberty.

`Montserrat-Bold.ttf` is also a **variable font whose default instance is
wght=100, Thin**, despite the filename. `--weight 900` is required or the mark
comes out thin while every printed model is Black.

## Regenerate

```sh
python3 tools/text_to_svg.py "OBC" \
    --font /root/.fonts/Montserrat-Bold.ttf --weight 900 \
    --width-mm 20 -o assets/brand/OBC.svg

python3 tools/text_to_svg.py "OnBrandCraftz" \
    --font assets/fonts/Caveat-Bold.ttf \
    --width-mm 80 -o assets/brand/OnBrandCraftz.svg
```

Needs `uharfbuzz` for kerning (both faces carry theirs in GPOS). Without it the
tool prints a warning and falls back to raw advances — it never silently ships
unkerned type.
