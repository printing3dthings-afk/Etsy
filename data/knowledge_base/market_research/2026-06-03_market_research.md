## 2. UNDERSERVED NICHES (OPPORTUNITY GAPS)

These niches show keyword presence in titles/tags but the top performers have **disproportionately low favorites relative to search signal**, indicating demand exists but the best listing hasn't been made yet.

---

### Gap #1 — Cottagecore / Forestcore Wall Art
**Signal:** "cottagecore decor" tag appears 10x; "forestcore" explicitly in a top performer title (owl listing, 1,441 favs); "gallery wall" + cottagecore crossover appears but is sparse in tags.
**Gap:** "cottagecore decor" tag at 10x frequency but no dedicated cottagecore gallery wall set appears in top 10. The owl listing proves the aesthetic converts, but it's a single item not a set.
**Opportunity:** A 4–6 piece cottagecore/forestcore gallery wall set with mushrooms, moths, pressed flowers, vintage botanical in muted earth tones. Low direct competition for *sets* in this aesthetic.
**Target favorites ceiling to beat:** ~218 (seascape gallery set) — very achievable.

---

### Gap #2 — Gym / Fitness SVG Bundles
**Signal:** Gym Motivation SVG bundle sits at 819 favorites with a $9.40 price point — strong performance. But "fitness" and "gym" appear **zero times** in top_title_words list beyond that one performer.
**Gap:** Only one gym/fitness listing broke into the top 10 performers, meaning the niche has proven buyers but almost no one is competing in it with optimized listings.
**Opportunity:** A gym/workout SVG bundle with motivational quotes, dumbbell silhouettes, and "strong mama" or "lift heavy be happy" style cuts. Cross-tag with sublimation for tumbler wraps.
**Target favorites ceiling to beat:** 819 — this is the floor, not the ceiling.

---

### Gap #3 — Nurse/Medical Sublimation (Non-Generic Designs)
**Signal:** "nurse" appears 50x in sublimation titles — very high. But the top performer (688 favs) is a basic flat design. The 3D inflated nurse design (453 favs) is a notably different execution.
**Gap:** The 3D/inflated puff style is clearly newer and already performing within 35% of the category leader despite being a more complex design. Most listings appear to be standard flat art.
**Opportunity:** 3D inflated/puff-style nurse tumbler wraps, or niche medical specialties (ICU nurse, ER nurse, pediatric nurse) rather than generic "nurse." Also: combining nurse identity with western or floral overlay is undetected in the data.
**Target favorites ceiling to beat:** 453–688.

---

### Gap #4 — Inspirational Quote Wall Art (Non-Generic / Niche Identity)
**Signal:** "inspirational" appears 24x in wall art titles, "quote" 40x, "inspirational quote" tag 15x. But the top quote performer has only 367 favorites — dramatically lower than botanical or dark academia performers.
**Gap:** Generic inspirational quotes are saturated and underperforming. However, the "Difference Maker Definition Print" (367 favs) uses a **specific identity frame** (teacher/helper/caregiver persona implied) rather than generic "you got this" content. That specificity is the gap.
**Opportunity:** Niche-identity definition prints — "Nurse Definition," "Dog Mom Definition," "Plant Lady Definition," "Kindergarten Teacher Definition" — styled minimally as printable wall art. These serve a gift-buying customer with high intent.
**Target favorites ceiling to beat:** 295–367.

---

### Gap #5 — Kawaii / Cute Animal Nursery Wall Art
**Signal:** "nursery" appears 50x in wall art titles; "nursery wall art" tag 14x; bunny nursery listing has 1,066 favorites. The kawaii/cute animal space is adjacent to OnBrandCraftz's existing kawaii planner category — a direct brand synergy.
**Gap:** The bunny nursery listing is the only clearly kawaii-style item in the top performers. Mickey Mouse (the other nursery item at 464 favs) is an IP violation risk. The cute/kawaii nursery niche for animals is **wide open for new designs**.
**Opportunity:** Kawaii animal nursery sets (bear, fox, bunny, duck) in muted sage/blush/cream palette as 4–6 piece gallery wall sets. Leverage the kawaii brand identity already established through the planner category.
**Target favorites ceiling to beat:** 625–1,066.
