⚙️ Setup & Connections

To connect your Etsy shop:
1. Open PowerShell in your ETSY folder
2. Run: python tools\etsy_oauth.py
3. A browser window opens — log in and authorize
4. Tokens are saved to .env automatically
5. Restart the town app
