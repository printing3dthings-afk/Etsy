# Desk Cable Rail (v2 of the Cable Hook)

Scott, 2026-11-12, picked form C of five: "a slim J-channel about 60 mm long that
screws under the desk edge and holds several cables in a row". The old Cable Hook
(a C-hook on an oversized plate, with two unexplained holes) was flagged by Scott for
delete-or-redo; this is the redo.

## Print
- `cable_rail.3mf`: one rail, **standing on its end**. 28 min, 9.1 g, no supports.
- **Why on its end:** laid flat either way, the J's curl hangs in the air. Standing,
  every wall is upright, and the screw holes and notches have pointed roofs.
- It's 60 mm tall on a small footprint: if your first layer isn't sticking, add a brim.
- **No maker's mark:** every face prints upright, and engraved letters on an upright
  face have ceilings the slicer props. The first draft's mark drew supports.

## Fit it
1. Hold it under the desk edge, the open gap toward the room or the wall.
2. Two #6 or #8 pan-head wood screws through the tab, or strong double-sided mounting
   tape on the tab's top.
3. Drop the cables in through the gap; lead any cable out through a notch.

## Checks (`checks/`)
- **Product gate:** pass. 0 support moves, 0 overhang perimeters, min wall 2.4.
  The first draft failed: its notches leaned the wrong way (53°, not 37°) and its
  maker's mark was on an upright face.
- **Print fidelity:** 0 flags. **Fragility:** nothing slender.
