# FACTS: Ladder Mouse bookmark, v1

Every number is from this folder's checks; the source is on each line.

## Version
- **v1**, 2026-11-11. The first version; nothing replaced.
- **Not printed yet.** Every image is a render or a toolpath simulation.

## What it is
A bookmark: a grey mouse climbing out of the book on a rope ladder. The ladder
goes between the pages; the top rung sits on the page edge and the mouse holds
it, peeking over the top.

## Numbers

| Ladder (inside the book) | 24 mm wide, 140 mm long, 1.6 mm thick | `ladder_mouse.scad` |
