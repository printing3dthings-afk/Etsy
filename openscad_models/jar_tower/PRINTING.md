# Stacking Jar Tower: printing

Printer-ready files in `checks/` (Bambu Studio, P1S 0.4 nozzle, PLA Basic, standard
0.20 mm layers, Textured PEI plate at 55 °C, no supports, **3 walls, 15% infill**: Scott's
settings). Any one colour per part. Re-sliced 2026-11-18; they open in Bambu Handy.

| print | file | how many | time each | filament each | on the plate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jar | `checks/tower_jar.gcode.3mf` | 4 | 1h 31m | 47.2 g | standing on its teeth |
| Cap | `checks/tower_cap.gcode.3mf` | 1 | 46m | 20.2 g | upside down, flat top on the plate |
| Foot | `checks/tower_foot.gcode.3mf` | 1 | 46m | 21.8 g | as it stands, skirt on the plate |

The full tower: about 7h 36m and 231 g. (At 2 walls, before 2026-11-18, it read 7h 54m and 204 g.)

Facts for posts and listings: `FACTS.md`.

Models to slice yourself: `stacking_jar_tower_jar.3mf`, `stacking_jar_tower_cap.3mf`,
`stacking_jar_tower_foot.3mf`.

The thread is the 0.2 mm one from the test print (mouth 1). How to put it together and
what to check: `guide/Stacking_Jar_Tower_Guide.pdf`.
