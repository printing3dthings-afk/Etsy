Voicing whistles: do they need support? (2026-11-12)

PrusaSlicer (tools/product_gate.py, supports on, threshold 45): 813 support moves, 0 overhang
perimeters -> the gate FAILS this test print. Its supports sit around the windway's exit,
where the 1 mm slot meets the window (support_map: interface tops at x 20.0-20.4, y -0.1-0.5,
z 18.2 on the first draft's layout).

Bambu Studio (the slicer the print uses), the same P1S 0.4 / PLA Basic profile with supports
switched ON (normal(auto), threshold 45): 0 support lines on the pair.
Control, same settings: a 10 mm post carrying a 40 x 20 mm slab -> 36 support lines, so the
setting was live.

Every surface was designed to print unsupported: chamber roof 37 deg from upright; windway,
window and finger holes with 37 deg pointed tops; the window and labium cut joined in one
arch peaking over the edge. The two slicers disagree only at the windway exit. The print
uses Bambu Studio with supports off (voicing_whistle_*.3mf). Reported, not hidden: the gate
result stands as FAIL for this test print.
