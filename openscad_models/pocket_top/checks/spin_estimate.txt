POCKET TOP UFO: SPIN ESTIMATE (a model, not a measurement)
  mass 3.34 g (sliced: saucer 2.60, stem 0.74); spin inertia 410 g mm2
  centre of mass 8.7 mm above the tip; it can't stay up below 56 rad/s (8.9 turns a second)
  the tip's contact patch about 0.041 mm across
  air drag: a free disc 34 mm across, laminar, both faces (Cochran's 3.87/sqrt(Re)); the
  dome, the lights and the slots add drag the model leaves out, so it's run at 1x and 2x.

  launch (turns/s)  tip friction mu  drag  -> spins for
                10              0.2   1.0x ->     7 s
                10              0.4   2.0x ->     3 s
                15              0.2   1.0x ->    29 s
                15              0.4   2.0x ->    14 s
                20              0.2   1.0x ->    42 s
                20              0.4   2.0x ->    21 s
                30              0.2   1.0x ->    59 s
                30              0.4   2.0x ->    30 s

  A good finger twist (assumed 15 to 30 turns a second, not measured) gives about 14 to 59 s.
  What limits it: air drag on the saucer for most of the spin; the 1 mm tip's friction near the end.
  The brief's 30 s is reachable from a firm twist in this model. Scott's timed spin decides it.
