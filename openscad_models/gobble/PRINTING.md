# Gobble — OnBrandCraftz

Original Hungry Monster Desk Buddy prototype: phone cradle, charging-cable opening, rear catchall and recessed OBC branding. Actual CAD previews, not photographs of a printed product.

## Files and quantities

Print one body, one tongue and one tray; print TWO each of eye, pupil and tooth. Nine pieces total. Individual STL and 3MF files are already in their intended print orientations. The assembly 3MF is VIEW ONLY; do not slice it as one object. The USDZ is for phone viewing. Individual 3MF files contain geometry, not a tuned Bambu printer profile.

Body prints on its broad rear face (172 × 210 mm footprint, 109 mm high). Tongue, tray and decorative pieces print on their flat undersides. Assembled height approximately 210 mm; footprint approximately 172 × 182 mm. Keep OBC.svg beside Gobble.scad to regenerate parts. OBC is recessed on the body's rear and the tongue/tray undersides, with the orientation correction requested for earlier builds.

## Prototype settings

Start with PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, three walls, five top/bottom layers and 15% gyroid infill. Use a 256 mm-class bed. Inspect your slicer's first layer and bridges before printing; temperature, cooling and adhesion depend on your filament and printer. Print eyes, pupils and teeth together with sufficient cooling time. No multicolor printer is required: colors are separate pieces.

## Assembly and fit

Dry-fit before gluing. Slide the tongue's rear platform into the low mouth slot; both angled supports face upward and rearward. Nominal slot clearance is 0.5 mm per side and 0.4–0.6 mm vertically. Glue the tongue at its insertion surfaces after a phone fit test. Fit and glue pupils into the eye recesses, then eyes into the body sockets. Glue the two teeth to the lower mouth corners as shown in the preview. Attach the tray centered at the back, with its bottom resting on the desk; allow a thin adhesive bond line. These are adhesive joints, NOT tested snap fits.

A rectangular 85 × 14 × 165 mm phone envelope at 15 degrees was checked for collision-free clearance. This does not certify all phones or cases, camera bumps, cable plugs or landscape use. The center cable opening is 14 mm wide. Check the actual phone's seating, retention, charging connector and stability before final assembly. Add thin self-adhesive rubber pads to the underside of both feet if needed, keeping them level.

## Before selling

This is a first physical-prototype candidate, not a production-qualified product. Print one complete sample. Check the OBC orientation in hand, edge finish, eye bonds, phone contact points, tipping resistance with your heaviest intended phone, repeated placement and cable access. Inspect every glued part after handling. Record filament, settings, actual material cost and print time before pricing. Small removable pieces mean this is a desktop accessory, not a children's toy. Keep away from heat and do not advertise food-contact use.
