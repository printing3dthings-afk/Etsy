# Reference photos (Scott, 2026-09-30)

Two painted 3D-printed haunted houses: someone else's designs. Scott showed
them for the Christmas cottages: "more shape to them ... like the reference
photos." They set the standard only; no shape, proportion or detail is
copied from them.

What they do that our first two cottages did not:

- **several masses**, not one box: a tower, a projecting entry, side wings
  and bays, each with its own roof;
- **roofs at several heights**, and roofs that flare, curve or cast bells;
- **a strong vertical accent**, a tower or turret, that makes the silhouette
  read at thumbnail size;
- **an entry that steps out**: a gabled porch on posts, with steps;
- **depth everywhere**: brackets under the eaves, frames and bars standing
  well off the wall, deep reveals;
- **lit, every window glows** (`ref_both_lit.jpg`), so openings spread over
  all the masses and heights.
