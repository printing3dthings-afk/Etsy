# Christmas village — price and keyword research (2026-10-08)

Etsy blocks direct searches from the build container (HTTP 403, both the
shop's `search_etsy` tool and a plain fetch), so this comes from web-search
snapshots of Etsy listings and Etsy "market" pages. Prices are what the
snapshots showed and change often; most digital listings run a permanent
"sale". Treat the numbers as a range, not a quote.

## What competitors charge

**Printed pieces (finished, shipped):**
- Single small 3D printed village pieces (a church, a moon village lamp): $20.00.
- "Cozy Cocoa Cottage", one 3D printed tealight cottage: $50.00, free shipping.
- Christmas village set with LED tealights (several houses and a church): $26.99
  (from $29.99), free shipping.
- 3D printed gingerbread luminary sets of 3 to 9 houses, tealight not included:
  about $27 for a larger set.
- Winter Moon Village tealight holder (a whole village on a crescent): $66.90.
- Vintage Department 56 Dickens houses with lights: $21.74 to $58.50.

**Files (STL/3MF downloads):**
- Single house: $1.49 to $4.99 on sale, $1.99 to $8.29 before the sale.
- Victorian gingerbread mansion centerpiece: $7.15 (from $11.00).
- Village sets: 2 designs $4.49; 5 houses $5.34; 12 tealight houses $11.69
  (from $17.99); a "100+ houses" pack $4.95.
- Licences vary: most are personal use only; a few advertise commercial use.

## How ours differ

- Bigger and more detailed than most: 86 to 164 mm tall, 4.5 to 9.5 hours each.
- A battery LED tealight ships with every printed building (several
  competitors say "tealight not included").
- Printed white, ready to paint; painted photos by Jessee, labelled as such.
- Files come as a four-colour 3MF ready for an AMS, plus STLs. Few competitor
  files mention multi-colour or 3MF.
- Each style is a matched set: one design language, one base, same scale.

## Price recommendation (Scott's decision)

**Printed, white, tealight included, free shipping.** Tiered by print time,
since that is the real cost:

| tier | buildings | price |
|---|---|---|
| small (about 4.5–5 h) | Victorian cottage, Gingerbread cottage, cocoa café, cookie clock tower | **$29.99** |
| medium (5.5–7 h) | sweet shop, Gingerbread Santa's workshop, turret house, clock tower, candy cane chapel, shop-house | **$34.99** |
| large (8–10 h) | townhouse, toy shop, coaching inn, church, Victorian Santa's workshop | **$39.99** |

Free shipping because Etsy ranks listings with US shipping over $6 lower, and
these all ship under a pound. Rough net at $29.99: about $3.30 in Etsy fees,
$5–7 postage, about $3 for filament, tealight and box, leaving about $17. At
$39.99, about $25. Check postage in Etsy's own calculator before setting.

**Files:** $5.99 per building; **$24.99 for a whole village bundle** (8
Victorian or 7 Gingerbread buildings, about half the single price). Above the
cheapest STLs on purpose: larger, tested, four-colour 3MF.
