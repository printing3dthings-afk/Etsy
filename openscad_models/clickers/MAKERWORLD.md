## Gingerbread Tin Clicker

**Title:** Gingerbread Tin Clicker: MX Switch Christmas Fidget

Press the gingerbread man down into his Christmas tin and he clicks. It's a
real keyboard switch, so it sounds and feels like one.

**What you need**
- 1 plate-mount Cherry MX switch, or any MX clone (Gateron, Kailh, Outemu).
  Blue or another clicky switch gives the loudest click.

**Printing**
- Three parts: the tin, its lid and the cookie. PLA, 0.2 mm layers, no
  supports, the orientation as it opens.
- Four colours if you have an AMS: red, white, brown and green. In one colour
  it still works and still reads, because all the icing and snowflakes are
  raised.
- About 31 g and an hour in one colour.
- If the cookie won't press onto the switch, print the loose cookie
  (`gingerbread_tin_clicker_loose_cap.3mf`).

**Assembly**
1. Push the switch down into the lid, through the cookie-shaped hole, until it
   snaps in.
2. Set the lid on the tin.
3. Press the cookie onto the switch.

The cookie sits in a nest shaped like him, so he goes down straight wherever
you press.

## Cookie Jar Peek-a-boo Clicker

**Title:** Cookie Jar Peek-a-boo Clicker: Gingerbread MX Switch Fidget

A gingerbread man pops out of the cookie jar, arms up. Push him back in and he
clicks, on a real keyboard switch.

**What you need**
- 1 plate-mount Cherry MX switch, or any MX clone. Blue or another clicky
  switch gives the loudest click.

**Printing**
- Three parts: the jar, its top and the gingerbread man. PLA, 0.2 mm layers,
  no supports, the orientation as it opens. He prints standing up.
- Four colours if you have an AMS: mint, white, red and brown. In one colour
  the raised COOKIES lettering and icing still read.
- About 35 g and an hour in one colour.
- If he won't press onto the switch, print the loose one
  (`cookie_jar_clicker_loose_cap.3mf`).

**Assembly**
1. Push the switch down the jar top's neck until it snaps in.
2. Set the top on the jar.
3. Drop him in and press him onto the switch.

He rides in the neck like a piston, so he goes down straight even if you push
one hand.
