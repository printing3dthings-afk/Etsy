## Giant Love Letter Valentine Mailbox

**Title:** Giant Love Letter: Classroom Valentine Mailbox with Name

A big standing envelope for the school Valentine's swap. Classmates post
their cards through the slot in the top. Lift the top off to empty it.

**Made for school**
- No small or loose parts, no magnets, no glue.
- Rounded everywhere hands go.
- The slot takes a standard kids' valentine in its envelope (2.75 × 4 in),
  even one with a sticker or a flat lollipop inside.
- Holds about 1.2 litres of cards.

**Printing**
- Two parts on one plate: the envelope and its lid. PLA, 0.2 mm layers, no
  supports, as the file opens.
- Four colours with an AMS: pink, red, white and dark plum. In one colour all
  the detail is still there, because it's all raised.
- The envelope prints standing on its base and the lid upside down.

**What's on it**
- The front is the address side: a stamp, a postmark, "To:" and the name.
- The back has the envelope's flaps and a heart wax seal.

180 × 62 × 124 mm.

## Hungry Love Monster Valentine Mailbox

**Title:** Hungry Love Monster: Classroom Valentine Box, Feed Me Valentines!

A chubby monster for the school Valentine's swap. His big toothy grin is the
card slot, and he's hugging a heart with your child's name on it.

**Made for school**
- No small or loose parts, no magnets, no glue.
- Rounded everywhere hands go.
- The mouth takes a standard kids' valentine in its envelope (2.75 × 4 in),
  even one with a sticker or a flat lollipop inside.

**Printing**
- Three prints, all PLA at 0.2 mm, no supports, as each file opens:
  - **The body** prints lying on its back.
  - **The face** prints flat, face up. Four colours with an AMS: lilac,
    white, red and dark. It reads in one colour too.
  - **The fur cap** prints upside down.

**Putting it together**
1. Slide the face down into the grooves at the front of the body.
2. Set the fur cap on top. It holds the face in.
3. To empty it, lift off the fur cap and tip the cards out.

160 × 120 × about 150 mm.
