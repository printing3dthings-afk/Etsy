## Title
Snowflake Folding Screen: 3-Panel Winter Desk Decor, No Supports

## Description
A little three-panel folding screen for a desk, shelf or mantel, with white
snowflakes inlaid flush in ice-blue panels. Stand it with the side panels angled
back.

**How it prints**
- Flat, in one go, the three panels side by side with their hinge knuckles
  already interleaved. A filament pin in each hinge holds them together.
- No supports. The snowflakes are the top three layers, so the face is smooth.
- Two colours: ice blue and white. An AMS makes the colour changes.
- About 2 h 10 m and 43 g of PLA on a P1S (Bambu Studio's own estimate).
- Laid flat it's 135 × 90 mm, so it fits the plate of any Bambu Lab printer.

**After printing**
- Cut two 88 mm pieces of 1.75 mm filament.
- Push one down into the small hole at the top of each hinge, all the way.
- Fold the side panels back and stand it up. The hinges turn about 100° each way;
  it doesn't fold flat.

**Included**
- The screen, with a 0.20 mm Standard profile for the P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle.
- A hinge test (24 min) to check the pin fit on your printer first.
- A PDF guide.

Images are renders of the model, not photos of a print.
