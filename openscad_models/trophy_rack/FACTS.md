# FACTS: Trophy Rack medallion, v2

Every number comes from this folder. The source is on each line (STANDARDS 15).

## Version
- **v2**, 2026-11-14 (`bb939e6`), then the tips in skin colour, Scott's pick (`4f0063e`).
- **What v2 changed from v1:**
  - folded ribbon tails;
  - a rounded band;

## What it is
- **Shield:** a black rim with a hanging tab (2 mm hole for a jump ring) and a yellow field.
- **Domes:** two skin-colour domes with tips.
- **Banner:** a cream ribbon across the bottom, with "TROPHY RACK" raised in black,
  following its curve.

## Print time and filament
- **Bambu Studio's own slice** at Scott's settings: wall loops 3, sparse infill 15% (Scott,
  2026-11-12). P1S 0.4, PLA Basic, 0.20 mm Standard, textured PEI, no supports, flat on
  its back. Re-sliced 2026-11-18; opens in Bambu Handy.
- **`checks/trophy_rack.gcode.3mf`: 2 h 58 m, 50.1 g.**

  | colour | filament |

- **Superseded:** 2 h 54 m, about 47 g. That was sliced 2026-11-14 at 2 walls, before the
  Handy fix.
