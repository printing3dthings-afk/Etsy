# Multipart product line — ideas (year-round)

Started 2026-10-27. Scott asked for a year-round line of multipart prints:
pieces printed separately and assembled, so each part can be its own colour
and things can move. He is picking five keepers; nothing gets modelled until
he has. Every idea assumes no supports on any part and fits from
`data/knowledge_base/3d_printing_expertise.md` (0.2–0.3 mm for slides and
joints, about 0.5 mm for snaps), with a fit check before he sees it.

## Keepers (picked by Scott)
